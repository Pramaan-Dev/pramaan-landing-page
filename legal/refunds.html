1. How credit works

  • Credit is prepaid, in US dollars. You add it by card through our payment provider.
  • Credit does not expire.
  • There are no plans, seats or subscriptions. The only repeat charges are rentals (numbers, inboxes, domains, campaigns, connected app accounts). They renew monthly from your credit until you release them.

2. What you are not charged for

  • A call that fails before doing any work.
  • Checking a price.
  • Calls made on your own provider accounts. The provider bills you directly.
  • A repeated call with the same idempotency key.

3. Caps on what you pay

  • A long job the source could not confirm is charged at most its estimate.
  • A mission is charged only for the steps that ran.
  • A call is never charged more than the amount held for it.

4. Starter credit

The $1 starter credit is a free gift, given once per person. It has no cash value and is never refunded or transferred. It is spent before purchased credit. [Confirm the order credit is spent in: OPEN QUESTION]

5. Refunds of purchased credit

Pick one before publishing:

  • Option A (recommended): unused purchased credit is refunded on request within [14 days] of the top-up. After that, it is refunded only when we close your account without cause, or when the law requires it.
  • Option B: unused purchased credit is refunded on request at any time, less payment fees.
  • Option C: purchased credit is not refundable, except where the law requires it (Naive's approach).

Used credit, rental months already started, and registration fees are not refunded.

6. Wrong charges

If you think a charge is wrong, email [CONTACT EMAIL] within [60 days]. If we got it wrong, we put the credit back.

7. Card disputes

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank. While a dispute is open, we may pause the workspace.
