Providers that carry out superpowers

Each superpower is carried out by one or more third-party providers. Which one depends on the call and changes over time. They receive only what that call needs and a hashed workspace tag. By category, they are:

  • people and company data providers;
  • telephony and messaging providers (phone numbers, SMS, voice);
  • email inbox and sending providers;
  • connected-app platforms (for apps you connect, such as a CRM or calendar);
  • social posting providers;
  • AI model providers;
  • web search, scraping and other public-data providers.

Customers who sign the DPA receive the full named list.

Changes

We will give 30 days' notice of a new subprocessor to builders who have signed the DPA. They can object.
