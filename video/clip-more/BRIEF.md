## Intent

An 18-second silent seamless loop for a Pramaan website section card ("Coming soon").
A calm run of four asks (4.5s each: type ~1.15s, send, card readable ~2.4s, short exit);
each shows a small preview card tagged COMING SOON. (Revised from 10s after review: cards were
only ~1.2s readable.)

## Customizations

- a) "Does ChatGPT recommend my business?" -> "Mentioned in 3 of 10 answers" +
  "Who they mention instead: 2 competitors".
- b) "Reply to my Google reviews" -> drafted reply snippet + "Posts after your OK".
- c) "Is this résumé true?" -> "Worked at Acme" (check) · "Degree" (check) · "Title" (question, "check").
- d) "Match my bank payments to invoices" -> "18 of 20 matched · 2 need a look".
- Loop back to empty ask pill.
- Deliverables: media/clip-more.mp4 (H.264 yuv420p faststart, no audio, < 1.2 MB),
  media/clip-more.webm (VP9), media/clip-more.jpg poster (frame 0).

## Notes

- Visual language copied from video/watch-it-work. No Lake Blue (no Yes buttons).
- "ChatGPT" and "Google" appear only because they are the user's own words in the ask
  (approved storyboard copy), not as provider claims.
- Min ~22px UI text, titles >= 40px.
