## Intent

A 10-second silent seamless loop for a Pramaan website section card ("Research" capability).
Shown at ~600px wide, so content is centered and large. Calm, editorial, no jargon.

## Customizations

- Beats: (0-2.6s) ask pill types "Research Acme before my meeting", send tap · (2.6-3.4s) ask
  rises and shrinks to a header · (3.4-6.8s) brief card: title "Acme Corp", three rows
  (What they do: Packaging for food brands · Recent news: Opened a plant in Pune · Key people:
  Jane Doe, VP Marketing), tag pill "NO OK NEEDED · JUST A LOOKUP" · (6.8-9.2s) drawn check +
  "Saved to your history" · (9.2-10s) fade back to empty ask pill.
- Deliverables: media/clip-research.mp4 (H.264 yuv420p faststart, no audio, < 1.2 MB),
  media/clip-research.webm (VP9), media/clip-research.jpg poster (frame 0).
