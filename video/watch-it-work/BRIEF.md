## Intent

A 12-second silent hero loop for the Pramaan website ("Watch it work" section). Autoplay, muted,
seamless loop. Shows a plain-words ask, Pramaan doing the job across sources, a single approval
tap, and a receipt-style proof card — then settles back to the empty ask box. Calm, editorial,
no jargon.

## Customizations

- Beats: (0–3s) "JUST ASK" label + ask pill typing "Research Acme before my meeting" with blinking
  cursor · (3–6s) Pramaan hub (ring+dot mark, serif "pramaan") with three pill nodes lighting one
  by one with thin lines + checks: "Web search", "Business data", "News" · (6–9s) approval card
  "Send the summary to your email?" with "Not now" (ghost) and "Yes" (Lake Blue #2b59d1, the only
  blue in the clip), tap pulse on Yes · (9–12s) receipt card "YOUR JOB · DONE" with asked /
  steps / checked / time / cost rows and "✓ Saved to your history"; then fade back to empty ask box.
- Deliverables: media/watch-it-work.mp4 (H.264, yuv420p, faststart, no audio, < 2 MB),
  media/watch-it-work.webm (VP9), media/watch-it-work.jpg poster.
