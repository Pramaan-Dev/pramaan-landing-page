If you run campaigns, you want to know what your competitor is saying and where. Which offers, which words, which landing pages, and how long they've been running. With Pramaan you name the competitor and get a report on everything they advertise, in one place. You can also group their ads into themes and set them next to your own.

## Why is it hard to see a competitor's ads?

Because their ads are spread out. Library ads live on each network's own ad archive, search ads show up only when you search the right words, and the landing pages are somewhere else again.

Checking by hand gives you a few ads from one day. You miss what changed last month, and you never see the whole picture.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You ask about one competitor. Pramaan gathers their library ads, their search ad copy, the keywords they pay for and the landing pages they send people to, and writes it up as one report. If you want themes instead of a list, it groups the ads. If you want a comparison, it sets their ads beside yours.

## What does it look like, step by step?

**You ask:** "Show me everything Fabrikam advertises, and tell me how it differs from our ads." (Fabrikam is a made-up competitor, used as an example.)

**Pramaan builds the full picture.** The [See everything a competitor advertises](/missions/ads.competitor.footprint) mission reports on library ads across three networks, plus their search ad copy, keywords and landing pages, in one report. Start here when you don't know where the competitor is active. Behind the report, Pramaan finds the competitor as a search advertiser, searches the ad libraries, reads each ad's detail, pulls the keywords and landing pages the competitor's domain pays for, checks its ad history, and writes the report.

**Pramaan groups the ads into themes.** [Research ads from a public ad library](/missions/ad.intel) searches a public ad archive. If you name an advertiser, you get that advertiser's ads. If you give a keyword, you get ads that match it. Either way, the ads are grouped into themes, so you see "free trial" and "switch and save" as patterns instead of a long list of single ads.

**Pramaan compares with yours.** [Compare your ads with a competitor's](/missions/ads.competitor.compare) takes your ads and the competitor's public ads and shows what each side runs and where they differ. This needs your own ad account connected, so Pramaan can read your ads. It reads your ads, searches the library for theirs, and writes the comparison.

**You decide what to change.** You spot the gap, such as an offer they push that you don't mention, and you take it to your next brief.

## What do I do with the report?

Take it to your next campaign brief. Note the offers they repeat, then check whether your own ads say anything about them. Look at the keywords they pay for and ask which ones you want to compete on. Look at the landing pages and note what each one asks a visitor to do.

You are looking for gaps, such as an offer they push that you never mention, and for searches where you have no ad at all.

## How do I phrase the ask?

Name the competitor by company name or domain. Say which markets matter to you, and say whether you want the full report or only the themes. If you don't know where the competitor is active, ask for the full report first.
