If you run paid campaigns, you know the Monday routine. You open the ad manager, sort by spend, and hunt for the ads that cost money and did nothing. With Pramaan you ask once, and it lists the ads, ad sets and campaigns that spent with no conversions, shows which ads are wearing out, and writes up the numbers.

## Why is wasted ad spend so hard to find?

Because the answer sits at three levels. A campaign can look fine while one ad inside it burns budget. An ad can look fine until you compare this week's clicks with last week's.

You end up clicking through campaigns, then ad sets, then ads, then exporting a sheet to compare days. If you run more than one account or currency, the sums get harder still.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You ask in plain words, and Pramaan reads your connected ad accounts and reports back. It checks campaigns, ad sets and ads for spend without conversions, and for clicks per impression that fell while spend held. The work is read-only. It does not pause, edit or delete anything.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "Which of our ads spent money last month and got no conversions?"

**Pramaan plans.** It picks the [Find ads that spent without results](/missions/ads.waste.find) mission. Pramaan lists your campaigns, ad sets and ads, then pulls the results for each.

**It finds the waste.** You get the ads, ad sets and campaigns that spent money and produced no conversions, with the spend shown in each currency. Money is never added across currencies.

**You ask a second question:** "Are any of the ads that still convert getting tired?"

**It checks for wear.** [Spot ads that are wearing out](/missions/ads.creative.fatigue) lists ads whose clicks per impression fell over recent days while spend held, with the daily numbers beside each one. You can see the trend yourself before you decide anything.

**You ask for the overview:** "Give me a spend report for the team."

**It writes the report.** [Report on ad spend and results](/missions/ads.performance.report) gives spend and results by campaign across your ad accounts, with observations on what the numbers show. The mission notes that nothing in it is advice, so the call on what to pause stays with you. To build it, Pramaan lists your ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets and ads, pulls the results, and writes the observations.

**Done, with proof.** Every row comes from your own accounts, so you can open the same ad in the ad manager and check it.

## What do I do with the list?

Open each ad in your ad manager first. An ad with no conversions may have a tracking problem, so check that conversion tracking was working for the period before you judge it. Then decide whether to pause it, change it or give it more time.

Read the wear list the same way. Falling clicks per impression with steady spend is the pattern the mission looks for. Compare it with any recent change to the ad before you replace it.

## How do I phrase the ask?

Name the period and the accounts, such as last month across all your connected accounts. Say whether you want the waste list, the wear list or the spend report. "No conversions" here means the ad platform reported spend and zero conversions for that period.

## How often should I run it?

Run the waste check at month end and the wear check each week, because wear is about the last few days. Each run is priced when you ask for it, so you can see the cost before you decide how often to repeat it.
