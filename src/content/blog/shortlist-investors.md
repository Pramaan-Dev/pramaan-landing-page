If you're a founder planning a raise, you know the early weeks. You read funding news, copy investor names into a sheet, and guess who writes checks at your stage. With Pramaan you name your company and get investors ranked from comparable funding rounds, with named partners and verified work emails where they're found. You can then benchmark your round against the market.

## Why does building an investor list take so long?

Fundraising already takes months, and the list is the first slog. Most lists start from a few famous names and a lot of hope.

The useful signal is who backed companies like yours at the stage you're at. That means reading funding history for many companies, one by one.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You give Pramaan your company, or one close to it. It finds similar companies, reads their funding rounds, and ranks the investors behind them. You get a shortlist built from real rounds, not from a generic top-100.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "Shortlist investors for a seed raise, based on companies like ours."

**Pramaan plans.** It picks [Shortlist investors for your raise](/missions/fundraising.investor.shortlist). It finds similar companies, then pulls their funding history.

**It ranks investors.** Investors are ranked from stage-matched comparable funding rounds. Pramaan then reads each investor's profile and portfolio.

**It adds people.** If you ask for it, the result includes named partners and verified work emails. Pramaan finds the people at each firm, looks up an email, and checks it.

**You check the round.** Ask: "Is the round we want typical?" [Benchmark a company's funding rounds](/missions/funding.round.benchmark) compares observed financing amounts, named investors and event dates for a company size, industry and place.

**Done, with proof.** The shortlist comes from rounds you can look up. If an investor ranks high, you can see the comparable rounds behind it.

## What should I put in my ask?

Name the company you want to be compared with, and the stage of the raise. The mission uses stage-matched rounds, so the stage decides which rounds count.

If you want people as well as firms, say so. Ask for named partners and verified work emails, and the result adds them for each firm.

## What does the mission do behind the scenes?

It works in four moves. It finds companies similar to yours and reads their funding history. It searches for investors and reads each profile and portfolio. It looks up people at each firm. Last, it finds a work email for each person and verifies it.

The partner and email moves are optional. Leave them out and you get the ranked firms and the rounds behind them.

## How do I use the benchmark?

The benchmark compares financing amounts, named investors and event dates for a company size, industry and place. Use it for one question: is the round you plan to ask for in line with rounds that were seen? If your target sits far from the others, you will know before an investor tells you.

## What else can your team ask for?

## Questions founders ask

**Can I do this without hiring someone?**
Yes. You ask in plain words, or your AI assistant asks for you.

**Will it email investors for me?**
No. It returns the list, and any outreach is yours.

**Where do the investor emails come from?**
Pramaan finds and verifies work emails through its data providers. You don't sign up with them.

**Is my data shared?**
The company details you enter are used to find comparable rounds. For how data is handled, see the [docs](https://docs.pramaan.dev).

**Can I start from a company that is not mine?**
Yes. You name your company or a close one, and Pramaan finds similar companies from there.

Before you send anything to a name on the list, open the rounds behind it and read the investor's profile. A firm that backed three companies like yours is a better first email than one that backed a famous company far from your space.
