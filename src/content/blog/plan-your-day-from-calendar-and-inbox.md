If you run a company or a team's operations, your morning probably starts the same way. Calendar in one tab, inbox in another, task list in a third. By the time you've sorted them, the morning has started without you. With Pramaan you ask for your day and get today's meetings, the mail that looks urgent and your open tasks in one view. You can then turn action items in email into tasks.

## Why does planning the day take so long?

Because the inputs live in three places and none of them tells you what matters. The calendar shows time. The inbox shows noise. The task list shows what you wrote down last week.

You are the only thing connecting them. Every morning you re-do that join by hand, and an action item buried in a thread slips through.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You ask once. Pramaan reads your connected calendar, a bounded sample of your mail and your open tasks, and returns a single view. A second mission sorts your newest mail by urgency. A third finds the action items in your email and creates tasks from them, after you approve.

## How do I sort the inbox by urgency?

The [Sort your newest mail by urgency](/missions/inbox.triage) mission reads your newest messages in one inbox. It sorts them by kind and urgency, and gives each a one-line summary. It adds a caution on mail that asks for money, credentials, secrecy or a rush. This one does not need approval. Nothing is sent or changed.

That caution matters. A message that says "pay this today, tell no one" gets flagged, however polite it sounds.

## How do I turn email into tasks?

The [Create tasks from your email](/missions/tasks.from.email) mission looks at a bounded sample of mail and extracts the exact wording of action items. It creates checked tasks in your task app after you approve.

Exact wording means it quotes what the sender wrote. It does not rephrase "can you look at this when you get a chance" into a deadline you never agreed to.

## What do I need connected first?

The day plan reads from your connected apps, so connect your calendar, your mail and your task app before you ask. Pramaan checks which apps are connected at the start. If one is missing, you know before anything is read.

The inbox sort is narrower: it reads the newest mail in one inbox and nothing else.

## A morning routine you can copy

1. Ask for the day plan before your first meeting.
2. Read the urgent list. Handle anything with a cost or a deadline first.
3. Ask for tasks from yesterday's mail, approve the ones that are real, and skip the rest.

The join between calendar, inbox and tasks is done for you, and you spend your time deciding.
