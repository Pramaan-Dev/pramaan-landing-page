If you run marketing for a brand, some of your best people are already talking about you, and you may not know who they are. With Pramaan you name your brand and get the accounts that post about it, a view of what's said across news and social right now, and your complaints and requests grouped with the exact posts behind them.

## Why is it hard to see who is posting about you?

Mentions are spread across social networks, discussion sites and the news. Each has its own search box, and each shows posts in its own order.

So you check a few by hand, miss the rest, and never get a list of the people who bring up your brand most. Complaints hide in the same pile.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You ask once, in plain words. Pramaan searches several sources for posts that name your brand, ranks the accounts by the reach each platform reports, and writes the themes with the posts behind them. It only reads public posts. It does not reply or post.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "Who is already posting about our coffee grinder, and what are they saying?"

**Pramaan plans.** It starts with [Find people already posting about your brand](/missions/advocates.find). It searches posts on X, TikTok, Instagram hashtags and Reddit, plus a brand-mentions source.

**You get a ranked list.** The result is the accounts already posting about your brand, ranked by reported reach within each platform, with the posts that name it. Reach is never added across platforms.

**You ask what's being said right now.** [Monitor what's said about a brand](/missions/brand.monitor) reads news and social, then returns the posts that name the brand, the volume per source, and themes that point to the posts behind them.

**You ask what's going wrong.** [Mine posts for complaints and requests](/missions/complaints.mine) groups product complaints, feature requests and praise from sampled posts and comments. Each group comes with exact excerpts from the source, so you can read the words people used.

**Done, with proof.** Every theme links back to real posts. If a theme looks off, you open the post and see for yourself.

## What do the missions read?

The advocate search reads posts on X, TikTok, Instagram hashtags and Reddit, plus a brand-mentions source. The monitor adds news results, Threads and YouTube. The complaints mission reads posts on Reddit and X and comments on YouTube and TikTok videos.

The complaints mission sorts what it finds into three groups: product complaints, feature requests and praise. Under each group you get exact excerpts, word for word.

## What do I do with the list?

Start with the top account on each platform and read its posts. You are looking for people who already like the product, and for a question you could answer in public.

Then read the complaint excerpts before you read any summary. Wording tells you more than a label. "Hard to clean" and "broke after a week" are both complaints, and they go to different teams.

## A weekly routine you can copy

1. Run the advocate search and note any account that is new to you.
2. Run the monitor and read the volume per source, to see where the talk is.
3. Run the complaints mission and send the excerpts to whoever owns the product.

The first run gives you a baseline. Later runs show what changed.
