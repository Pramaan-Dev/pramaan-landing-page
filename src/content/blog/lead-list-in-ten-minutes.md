If you run sales, you know the week: a spreadsheet, ten browser tabs, and a lot of copying and pasting before a single email goes out. Pramaan does that part for you. You ask for the people you want to reach, and you get back a clean list with work emails and a first draft for each person.

## Why does building a lead list take so long?

Because it is five jobs pretending to be one. You find the companies, find the right person at each, find their email, check the email works, then write something worth reading.

Each step lives in a different tool. Most of the time goes on moving data between them.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You describe the list once, in plain words, and Pramaan does all five steps. You only step in to approve before anything is sent.

## What does it look like?

**You ask:** "Find 50 heads of sales at fintech companies in London and draft a first email to each."

**Pramaan plans:** three small jobs. Build the list, find and check the emails, write the drafts.

**Picks the tools:** it runs the [Build a list of people to contact](/missions/lead.list.build) mission, then [Find a lead's work email and phone](/missions/lead.contact.find) for each person, then [Clean an outreach contact list](/missions/outreach.list.hygiene) to drop bad addresses.

**Drafts:** [Draft a first email to a lead](/missions/outreach.draft) writes one note per person, using what it found about their company.

**Asks your OK:** nothing is sent. You read the drafts, change what you like, and decide.

## How do I stay in control?

Pramaan never contacts anyone without your yes. Anything that sends a message or moves money stops and shows you exactly what will happen first.

You pay from a prepaid balance, only for what runs, and you see the price before a job starts.
