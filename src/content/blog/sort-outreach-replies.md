If you send outbound, the replies come back mixed. A real "yes, send me times" sits under three out-of-office notes and a polite no. With Pramaan you ask it to sort the replies to a sequence, and the hot ones come out on top. For the interested prospects you get a drafted answer waiting in your own inbox. You read the draft, change it if you like, and send it yourself.

## Why do good replies get buried?

An inbox sorts by time and knows nothing about intent. A reply that could become a meeting arrives at 9:02 and is buried by 9:40.

Slow answers cost you. A prospect who said yes on Tuesday and heard back on Friday has cooled down. Also, people who asked to stop need to be found and left alone, and that is easy to miss by eye.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You point Pramaan at the replies to a sequence. It reads each thread and sorts the replies into groups: interested, not now, wrong person, out of office and unsubscribe. It also lists the people who asked to stop. Then you ask for a draft answer to any reply.

## What does it look like, step by step?

**You ask:** "Sort the replies to my fintech sequence. Show me who's interested first."

**Pramaan sorts the replies.** The [Sort replies to your outreach](/missions/outreach.replies.triage) mission reads your threads and the contacts in the sequence. It sorts every reply into one of five groups: interested, not now, wrong person, out of office and unsubscribe. It also lists the people who asked to stop, so you know who not to write to again. To do it, Pramaan lists your threads, opens each one, matches it to the contacts in the sequence, and reads the reply.

**You read the hot ones.** The interested group is the one to read first. Each person in it has already answered you, so reply to these before anything else.

**Pramaan drafts an answer.** [Draft a reply to a prospect](/missions/outreach.reply.draft) reads the thread and your context, then writes an answer and checks it sentence by sentence. The draft is stored in the same inbox, with the recipient and subject taken from the thread. It is a draft only. Nothing is sent. Behind the draft, Pramaan reads the thread, looks up the company, writes the answer, checks it and saves it.

**You send it.** You edit the draft in your own mailbox and send when you're ready.

## How do I ask for the sort?

Name the sequence and the inbox it ran from. Say which group you want to see first. If you only care about the hot replies, say so and start there.

## Where does the draft end up?

In the same inbox as the thread, as a new draft. The recipient and the subject come from the thread's headers, so you don't retype them. Open it where you normally write mail, change the wording, and send it from there.

## How should I work through the sorted list?

Start with interested, then wrong person, because a name from a wrong-person reply is a warm way in. Handle unsubscribe before your next send, so nobody who asked to stop gets another email. Leave not now and out of office for last, and set a date for each.

Ask for a draft only on the replies you plan to answer. Pramaan writes it from the thread, so it picks up what the prospect said.
