If you run marketing, more of your buyers now ask an AI assistant before they search. Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the work of getting your brand named and your pages cited in those answers. This guide explains what AEO is, how it differs from SEO, and how to measure and improve where you show up.

**Quick summary**

- AEO is about being mentioned and cited in AI answers, such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI overviews.
- Start with the questions your buyers ask, then measure how often answers name you.
- Find the pages the answers cite instead of yours, and see what those pages cover that yours don't.
- Fix your pages, then measure again each month.

## What is answer engine optimization?

Answer engine optimization is making your brand and pages easy for AI answer tools to find, trust and cite.

An answer engine is any tool that replies with a written answer instead of a list of links. It reads pages across the web, writes a reply, and often lists the sources it used. If your page is one of those sources, you get named, and sometimes clicked.

Here's an example: a buyer asks "What's the best scheduling software for small dental clinics?" The answer names three products and cites four pages. AEO is the work of getting your product named in that answer, with your page among the four.

## Is AEO the same as SEO?

No, though they overlap. SEO (search engine optimization) aims to rank your page in a list of results. AEO aims to get your brand named inside a written answer.

## Why does AEO matter?

- **Buyers read the answer and stop.** If you're not in it, they may never see your site.
- **Rivals get named in your place.** An answer that lists three competitors shapes the shortlist.
- **Answers describe your brand for you.** You want to know what they say, and which pages they rest on.
- **It's measurable.** You can count mentions and citations, and see them change after you fix a page.

## How do you do answer engine optimization?

Work through it in five steps.

### 1. List the questions your buyers ask

Write down the questions a buyer would ask before choosing a product like yours. Use their words, and include comparisons ("X vs Y") and "best for" questions.

"[Research what people ask AI tools](/missions/aeo.prompt.research)" finds the questions people ask AI tools about a topic, with how often each is used. When you ask, it adds related web searches.

### 2. Measure how often you're mentioned

Run your questions through several answer tools and count how often your brand comes up.

"[Measure your brand in AI answers](/missions/brand.visibility)" asks a sample of AI answer tools your set of questions. It reports how often the answers mention your brand, which sites they cite, and estimated mention figures for your domain. It's a snapshot of one moment.

### 3. Find the pages cited instead of yours

See which pages the answers lean on for your topic, and whether any of them are yours.

"[Find pages AI answers cite instead of yours](/missions/aeo.citation.gap)" lists the pages and domains AI answer tools cite most for a topic. It checks them against your own pages and shows which of yours are among the most cited and which are not.

### 4. Plan the page fixes

Read the cited pages and note what they cover that yours don't: a clear definition, a comparison table, a price range, a direct answer near the top.

"[Plan page fixes to appear in AI answers](/missions/aeo.fix.plan)" does this for the questions where your brand is missing. It reads the pages the answers cite and proposes changes to your page based on what they cover and yours lacks. It proposes changes. It doesn't edit your site.

### 5. Measure again every month

Answers change as tools update and new pages appear. Track the same questions over time.

"[Track brand mentions in AI answers by month](/missions/brand.visibility.history)" shows your brand's estimated mentions in AI answers month by month, with a total for the months returned.

## What mistakes should you avoid?

- **Checking one tool once.** Answers differ between tools and change over time. Use several tools and measure monthly.
- **Testing only your brand name.** Buyers ask about the problem, not your name. Test the questions they ask before they've heard of you.
- **Burying the answer.** Pages that answer the question in the first lines are easier to quote.
- **Writing for machines.** Clear pages that answer a real question work for people and answer tools alike.
- **Ignoring the sites that get cited.** Review sites and guides cited in your space are worth being listed on.
