If you run sales or you're a founder doing your own outbound, you know the week. A spreadsheet, ten browser tabs, and a lot of copying before a single email goes out. With Pramaan you describe the people you want to reach and see how many match before you pay. You get back a list with work emails, and a first draft for each person waits in your own inbox.

## Why does building a lead list take so long?

Because it is five jobs pretending to be one. You find the right companies, find the right person at each, find their email, check the email works, then write something worth reading.

Each job lives in a different tool. Most of the time goes on moving rows between them, not on selling.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You describe the list once, in plain words, and Pramaan runs the jobs in order. You decide how big the list is before anything is paid for, and nothing goes out to a prospect until you send it yourself.

## What does it look like, step by step?

**You ask:** "Find heads of sales at fintech companies in London. Show me how many there are first."

**Pramaan sizes the search.** The [Build a list of people to contact](/missions/lead.list.build) mission first counts how many people match, without returning anyone. Then it shows one page of sample profiles with names partly hidden, so you can check it found the right kind of person. You pay a small flat fee for that page.

**You set the size.** If the sample looks right, you say how many people you want. Pramaan exports that many rows, with work emails and mobile numbers where they're known. You pay per person returned, up to the cap you set.

**It fills the gaps.** Some people come back without an email. For them, [Find a lead's work email and phone](/missions/lead.contact.find) looks for an address on the company's own domain. It labels each one with what the mailbox check said. You can see which addresses the check confirmed and which it couldn't.

**It drafts the first email.** [Draft a first email to a lead](/missions/outreach.draft) writes one note per person from their job title, their company and the company's recent news. Each sentence is checked against what was found. The draft lands in an inbox you already use. It never sends.

**You decide.** You read the drafts, edit what you like, and send from your own mailbox when you're ready.

## How do I stay in control?

Every email in this flow stays a draft in your inbox. Pramaan does not send messages, place calls, move money or delete anything without your explicit yes.

You also control the spend. Pramaan runs on a prepaid balance. The count is shown before the export, the export stops at the number you chose, and you only pay for what runs.

## What else can my team ask for?

- "Research Acme before my call on Thursday." This runs [Research a company](/missions/company.research): their register record, recent news, site pages and a written summary, with sources.
- "Draft a three-step follow-up for this lead." This runs [Draft a three-step email sequence](/missions/outreach.sequence.draft). All three emails are saved as drafts.
- "Draft a reply to this prospect's question about pricing." This runs [Draft a reply to a prospect](/missions/outreach.reply.draft). It reads the thread and saves the answer as a new draft in the same inbox.

## Questions sales teams ask

**Do I need a developer?**
No. You ask in plain words, or your AI assistant asks for you.

**Will it email people on its own?**
No. The drafts sit in your inbox. Sending is always your call.

**Where do the emails come from?**
Pramaan looks them up from data providers, so you never sign up with them yourself. Each one is labeled with the result of a mailbox check.

**What does it cost?**
You pay per use from a prepaid balance. You see the match count before you export, and the export never goes past the number you set.
