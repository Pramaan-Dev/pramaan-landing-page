If you sell to people who use LinkedIn, the likes and comments on the right post are a warm list. Someone who reacts to a post about your problem has told you they care. With Pramaan you give it a post link and get back the people who reacted or commented. You can also set up a watch on a profile, so new engaged leads are collected for you as they appear.

## Why is this list so hard to get by hand?

Because LinkedIn shows you a few names at a time. You scroll the reactions, click through profiles, and copy names into a sheet. For a post with hundreds of reactions, nobody finishes.

The list also has a short life. People who engaged last week are warmer than people who engaged last quarter, and you rarely get to them in time.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You paste the link to one public post. Pramaan returns the people who reacted to it or commented on it. You can ask it to read their public profiles too, so each row has a title and a company. For ongoing work, you set up a watch on a profile and read the leads it captures.

## What does it look like, step by step?

**You ask:** "Who reacted to or commented on this post about hiring SDRs? Include their titles."

**Pramaan lists the people.** The [Find who engaged with a LinkedIn post](/missions/lead.engagers.find) mission reads the reactions and the comments on one post. It returns the people behind them. Reading their public profiles is a separate option, so you choose whether you want titles and companies on each row. Pramaan reads the reactions, then the comments, then each person's public profile if you asked for it.

**You pick who fits.** Filter by title or company. A head of sales who commented is a better lead than a student who liked.

**Pramaan watches a profile.** [Watch a profile for engaged leads](/missions/lead.engagement.watch) creates a paid watch on a profile. It lists your existing watches, creates one for the profile, and reads the work leads captured so far. You read them when you want. This sets up something that keeps running, so check the quote before you start.

**You reach out with a reason.** "I saw you commented on Priya's post about SDR hiring" is a real opener. (Priya is a made-up name, used as an example.)

## How should I write to someone who engaged?

Say what you saw and keep it short. Name the post, mention what they said if they commented, and ask one question about the topic. Leave the pitch for the second message.

The draft mission below saves a first email you can start from. Read it, cut anything that sounds like a template, and send it yourself.

## When should I use the watch instead of one post?

Use a single post when you want a list now, from a post you have already seen. Use the watch when a profile posts often and you want new engaged leads without checking by hand. The watch is paid and ongoing, and a single post is one run, so start with the post.

## How do I set up the ask?

Paste the full link to the post. Say whether you want profiles read, which gives each row a title and a company. Say which titles you care about, so you can filter the list when it comes back.
