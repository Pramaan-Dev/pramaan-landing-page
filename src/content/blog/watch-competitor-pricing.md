If you're a founder, you probably check a competitor's pricing page when you remember to, and you can't tell what changed since last time. With Pramaan you give it the address, approve a schedule, and read the plans before and after each change.

## Why is it hard to keep up with a competitor's pricing?

Pricing pages change without notice. A plan gets renamed, a limit moves, a price shifts, and you find out from a customer.

Checking by hand means comparing a page against your memory of it.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You point Pramaan at the page. After you approve, it checks on a schedule and keeps what the plans looked like before and after each change. You read the changes when you want to, instead of remembering to look.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "Watch our main competitor's pricing page and tell me when the plans change."

**Pramaan plans.** It picks [Watch a competitor's pricing page](/missions/pricing.change.watch). It reads the page, pulls out the plans, and sets up the scheduled check.

**It asks for approval.** This mission needs approval. Pramaan shows you exactly what it will watch and how often, and waits for your approval before the schedule starts. Nothing is set up until you approve.

**It checks on schedule.** From then on, Pramaan checks the page and records each change with the plans before and after.

**You read the changes.** You ask: "What changed on their pricing page?" The result shows the plans before and after each change.

**Done, with proof.** The before and after come from the page itself, so you can open it and compare.

## What does the mission do under the hood?

The mission works in four moves. It takes inventory of the page, pulls out the plans, creates a check that repeats on an interval, and reads back the changes that check finds. The approval comes before the check is created.

The pulled-out plans are the useful part. Instead of two screenshots to squint at, you get the plans as a list, so a change in a plan name or a price is easy to spot.

## What do I do when a plan changes?

Open their page and confirm the change yourself. Then decide whether it affects you: a price move may matter to a deal in progress, while a renamed plan may matter only to your sales deck.

If you want more than the pricing page, the page-watch mission can follow one page, a sitemap or a set of facts. It also needs approval, and it writes a sentence quoting each change.

## Who should see the changes?

Send the before and after table to whoever answers pricing questions from customers, and to whoever writes your own price page. They hear about a competitor's move from you, with the page link attached, instead of from a prospect on a call.

Keep the old results. Over a few weeks they show how often this competitor changes its plans, which is useful when you plan your own next price change.

## What should I check before I approve?

Look at three things in the approval screen: the page address, how often it will check, and the maximum price. A schedule repeats, so the frequency is what drives the cost.

If the address is a landing page rather than the pricing page, correct it before you approve. The plans can only be pulled from the page you point at.

## Questions founders ask

**Can I do this without hiring someone?**
Yes. You ask in plain words, or your AI assistant asks for you.

**Does it start watching on its own?**
No. It waits for your approval before the schedule starts.

**How often does it check?**
The schedule is part of what you approve. You see the frequency before it starts.

**Will the competitor know?**
Pramaan reads the public page. It does not contact the competitor.

**Can I watch a page that isn't about pricing?**
Yes, with the page-watch mission. It follows one page, a sitemap or a set of facts, and needs approval too.
