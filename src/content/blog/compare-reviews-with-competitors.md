If you own a product or a place customers review, you want to know how you stack up against the competition. Pramaan puts your ratings, review counts and review themes next to those of your local competitors. For apps, it maps the competing apps and the search terms they share with yours. You get the comparison in one result.

## Why is comparing reviews so tedious?

Every competitor has its own page, and star ratings alone say little. A 4.4 from 40 reviews and a 4.4 from 4,000 reviews are different facts.

The useful part is what customers praise and complain about at each place. To get it, someone has to read the reviews, one page at a time, and keep notes. Most teams do that for one rival, once, and the notes go stale.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You name your business and Pramaan finds the local competitors. It reads the reviews for each one and reports ratings, review counts and the themes customers raise. You see what rivals are praised for and where you could do better.

This works for businesses with a listing on a maps service. Apps use a different mission, covered below.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "Compare Corner Roast Cafe with the other coffee shops nearby." (Corner Roast Cafe is a made-up name for this example.)

**Pramaan plans.** The [Compare your reviews with local competitors](/missions/reviews.competitor.compare) mission runs three steps: it searches the map for businesses like yours in the same area, reads the reviews on the map listings, and writes a summary of what customers praise and complain about at each.

**It picks the tools.** Each step is a separate superpower, a single action with a fixed request and result. You never choose or sign up with a source.

**You see the proof.** The result holds ratings, review counts and the themes for your listing and each competitor. The ratings and counts come straight from the listings.

**You decide.** Choose one gap to close first. Pramaan compares, and the call on what to fix stays with your team.

## How should I read the comparison?

Read the ratings and the counts together, then read the themes.

<tr><th>You see</th><th>What to check</th></tr>

</table>

## How do I map competing apps?

Use the [Map competing mobile apps](/missions/app.competitor.map) mission. It finds the apps that compete with yours on the App Store and Google Play. It then reads each store's own ranking counts and the search terms the apps share with yours.

This mission maps the field. It does not compare app reviews side by side. For what users say about your app in public, use the reputation report listed below.

## Questions product teams ask

**Which review sites does it read?**
The local comparison reads map listings and their reviews. The reputation report covers Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot, maps and app stores.

**Does it work for apps?**
The app mission maps competing apps and their shared search terms. It does not compare app reviews side by side.

**Can it watch a competitor for us?**
Yes, for a page. [Watch a page for changes](/missions/page.change.watch) starts a scheduled check on one page, sitemap or set of facts, then reads recent changes with a sentence quoting each. It needs approval, so it waits for your approval before it starts.

**Do I need a developer?**
No. You ask in plain words, or your AI assistant asks for you.
