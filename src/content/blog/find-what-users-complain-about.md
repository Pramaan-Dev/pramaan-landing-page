If you run a product, your users are already telling you what is wrong. They say it in posts and comments, spread across sites you don't have time to read. Pramaan samples public posts about your product or topic, groups the complaints and requests into themes, and shows an exact excerpt for each one, so you can see what is behind a theme.

## Why is user feedback so scattered?

People don't use your feedback form. They post on Reddit, on X, under YouTube videos and on TikTok. Each site has its own search, and none of them agree on how to count a complaint.

So the loudest voice in the last thread you read becomes your roadmap.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You name the product or topic. Pramaan reads a sample of public posts and comments across several sites, then groups what it finds. Each theme names the posts it came from.

You are looking at a sample, not every post online. Read the excerpts to judge how much weight a theme deserves.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "What do people complain about with note-taking apps, and what do they ask for?" (A made-up question for this example.)

**Pramaan mines the complaints.** The [Mine posts for complaints and requests](/missions/complaints.mine) mission searches public posts on Reddit and X, and reads comments on YouTube and TikTok videos. It groups product complaints, feature requests and praise, with exact excerpts from the sources.

**Pramaan finds the questions.** The [Find questions people ask about a topic](/missions/community.questions.find) mission looks through Reddit and communities, X and Threads. It returns the questions people ask, which you can read as gaps in your product, your docs or your onboarding.

**Pramaan listens wider.** The [Listen to what people say about a topic](/missions/topic.listen) mission covers news and social, including posts, comments on the top posts, and the volume per source. Its themes name the posts behind them.

**You read the excerpts.** Open a few quotes under each theme. A theme backed by ten different people means more than one backed by a single long thread.

**You decide.** Pick the two or three themes worth testing with your own users. Pramaan finds the signals, and you choose what to build.

## Questions product teams ask

**Which sites does it read?**
The complaints mission searches Reddit and X, and reads YouTube and TikTok comments. The questions mission covers Reddit, X and Threads. The topic mission adds news.

**Is it every post about my product?**
No. It reads a sample. Use the excerpts to judge how much each theme counts.

**Can it watch a competitor for us?**
Yes, for some jobs. Ask for the competitor by name and Pramaan picks a matching mission.

**Will it post or reply for us?**
No. These missions only read public posts and report what they found.

**Do I need a developer?**
No. You ask in plain words, or your AI assistant asks for you.
