If you run an online store, you know which listings underperform. The title is vague, the bullets repeat each other, and the competitor's page reads better. Pramaan reads your listing and suggests a better title and bullet points, checked line by line against what your own listing says. For a whole catalog, it rewrites titles and descriptions and keeps the old text so you can undo a change.

## Why do listings go stale?

Nobody has time to rewrite hundreds of them. A listing gets written once, in a hurry, and then it sits there.

Shoppers skim. A weak title and soft bullets lose the click before the product gets a chance, and the team rarely finds out which listings are the weak ones until sales dip.

## What changes with Pramaan?

You point Pramaan at a listing. It reads your listing, looks at top sellers and keyword ideas as style examples, and drafts a new title and bullets. Every claim is checked against your own listing, so it doesn't invent features.

For a catalog in your store, a second mission rewrites titles and descriptions, and only for the fields you name.

## How does it work, step by step?

**You ask:** "Improve the listing for our stainless steel water bottle." (A made-up product for this example.)

**Pramaan plans.** The [Improve a marketplace listing](/missions/product.listing.improve) mission has four steps. It reads your product page, searches for similar products, pulls keyword ideas, and then writes the new copy.

**It picks the tools.** Each step is a superpower, a single action with a fixed request and result. The similar products and the keyword ideas are only style examples. Pramaan does not copy a rival's claims.

**You see the proof.** You get a suggested title and bullet points. Each line is checked against your own listing, so the facts stay yours.

**Nothing is written back.** This mission only suggests. You copy what you like into your store.

The suggested lines only work if your own listing says the same things. If your page never mentions 750 ml, the check has nothing to support that line, so you should not publish it.

The cleanest way to use the result is to read it next to your own page and keep only the lines you can back up. Add a missing fact to your listing first, and the next suggestion can use it.

## How do I improve a whole catalog?

The [Improve product titles and descriptions](/missions/catalog.enrich) mission works on products in one of your catalogs. It has three steps: it lists the products, writes new copy from each product's own listing, and writes it back.

It writes back only the fields you name. It also returns the values from before, so a change can be undone. Start with ten products, read the new copy, and widen it if you like what you see.

The catalog mission works on a catalog connected to Pramaan. Check your store setup before a large run.

No. These missions work on titles, bullets and descriptions, not prices.

The listing mission reads marketplace listings, with Amazon among its sources. Ask for your marketplace by name to see what fits.
