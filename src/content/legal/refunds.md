<strong>Effective date:</strong> 19 October 2026

## 1. How credit works

- Credit is prepaid, in US dollars. You buy it by card from Dodo Payments, which sells it as merchant of record and handles tax and receipts.
- <strong>Credit does not expire.</strong>
- There are no plans, seats or subscriptions. The only repeat charges are rentals (numbers, inboxes, domains, campaigns, connected app accounts). They renew monthly from your credit until you release them.

## 2. What you are not charged for

- A call that fails before doing any work.
- Checking a price.
- Calls made on your own provider accounts. The provider bills you directly.
- A repeated call with the same idempotency key.

## 3. Caps on what you pay

- A long job the source could not confirm is charged at most its estimate.
- A mission is charged only for the steps that ran.
- A call is never charged more than the amount held for it.

## 4. Starter credit

The $1 starter credit is a free gift, given once per person. It has no cash value and is never refunded or transferred. It sits in the same balance as purchased credit.

## 5. Refunds of purchased credit

- <strong>Purchased credit is not refundable, except where the law requires it.</strong>
- Dodo Payments' own refund rules, as merchant of record, also apply.
- If you close your workspace, unused credit is forfeited, unless the law requires otherwise.
- Used credit, rental months already started, and registration fees are not refunded.

## 6. Wrong charges

If you think a charge is wrong, email support@pramaan.dev within 60 days. If we got it wrong, we put the credit back.

## 7. Card disputes

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank. While a dispute is open, we may pause the workspace.
