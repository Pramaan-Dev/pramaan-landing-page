# Run "Creator-led growth in 90 days" with Pramaan

The skill says what to do. This page says which Pramaan superpowers do each step. Setup (key, MCP, SDK or HTTP): https://www.pramaan.dev/skill.md

This is a plan. Most of it needs no superpower.

## Step by step

| Skill step | Pramaan id | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30, week 2: source 8 to 12 creators | `linkedin.search.posts`, `linkedin.user.profile` | Who posts on the ICP's problem, and what they do. |
| Days 1 to 30, week 2: check audience-ICP fit | `linkedin.post.comments` | Share of commenters with ICP titles. |
| Days 31 to 60: read results per creator | `campaign.posts.report` | Views, likes and comments per post. Qualified clicks come from the user's own tracking. |
| Days 61 to 90: second cohort | `linkedin.search.posts`, `linkedin.user.profile` | Match the winner profile. |

The agent does the ICP, terms, tracking setup, briefs, the cut, the playbook and the forecast itself.
