# Missions: Research

Deep research, academic papers and answers that cite their sources.

- `reading.list.build`: Build a reading list from seed papers. Grow a bounded reading list from positive and negative DOI seeds, with separate recommendation ranks and compatible-space closeness. (steps: papers.recommend, papers.recommend-set, papers.embedding.get, text.rerank)
- `research.table.build`: Build a research table. Research the caller's named fields for each row in one tracked batch, keeping each cell's sources and reported confidence. (steps: research.schema.suggest, research.tier.suggest, research.sources.extract, research.group.status, research.group.events, research.group.row)
- `source.credibility.check`: Check how far to trust a source. How much weight a cited page deserves, as figures: its site's authority and spam scores, the site's and page's authority over time, and the page's details. No verdict on the claim. (steps: web.url.metrics, web.spam-scores.list, web.page.metadata, web.domain-rating.history, web.url-rating.history)
- `answer.compare`: Compare answers to one question. One question asked of several answer sources, with where they agree and where they differ. (steps: web.answers, aeo.chatgpt.answer, aeo.perplexity.answer, aeo.google.answer, aeo.claude.answer, aeo.brave.answer, llm.complete)
- `fact.check`: Fact-check a claim. A claim checked against several pages, for and against, with no overall verdict. (steps: web.search, web.read, llm.complete)
- `paper.find`: Find a paper by title. Find the closest paper title, verify its public identifier and offer matching metadata and public copies. (steps: papers.title.match, papers.search-filtered, papers.entities.suggest, papers.get, papers.open-access.get)
- `venue.find`: Find journals and conferences for a topic. Find source-ranked venue candidates by topic, identify them by ISSN and report dated recent topic papers separately from undated ones. (steps: papers.venues.search, papers.venue.get, papers.search-filtered)
- `funder.papers.find`: Find papers by funder. Find papers with the exact funder acknowledgement and keep publication and open-access readings separately. (steps: papers.funders.search, papers.funder.get, papers.search-filtered, papers.open-access.get)
- `dataset.find`: Find public datasets on a topic. Shortlist public datasets on a topic, with each one's providers, formats and licence read from its own page. (steps: google.datasets.search, google.dataset.detail, llm.complete)
- `quote.source.find`: Find where a quote came from. Where a quote or sentence appears: candidate pages found by searching the exact phrase, read, and matched to the wording in code. The earliest dated match is reported as the earliest found, not as the original. (steps: web.search, web.read)
- `topic.brief`: Get a short sourced answer. A short sourced answer to a question, each point tied to the pages it came from. (steps: web.search, web.read, text.rerank, llm.complete)
- `author.publications.report`: List a researcher's publications. Read a public researcher's publications, co-authors and dated affiliations using an exact public identifier, without scores. (steps: papers.authors.search, papers.author.get, papers.author.works, papers.authors.list)
- `topic.listen`: Listen to what people say about a topic. What people say about a topic across news and social: the posts, comments on the top posts, the volume per source, and themes that name the posts behind them. (steps: google.serp.news, x.search.posts, threads.search.posts, tiktok.search.videos, youtube.search.videos, instagram.search.reels, youtube.video.comments, tiktok.video.comments, x.post.comments, llm.complete)
- `papers.bulk.lookup`: Look up many papers or authors. Look up up to 500 papers or 1,000 public authors as one job, retaining every caller id and each row's failures. (steps: papers.get-bulk, papers.author.get-bulk)
- `paper.citation.map`: Map a paper's citations. Build a bounded two-level directed citation graph with original citing contexts and explicit unread edges. (steps: papers.get, papers.references.list, papers.citations.list)
- `topic.landscape`: Map the main players on a topic. The main players, sources or schools of thought on a topic, grouped, each with the page it comes from. A name is kept only when it occurs in its page's text; a model only groups and labels the kept names. (steps: web.deep.search, web.similar, web.read, text.entities.extract, llm.complete)
- `institution.research.profile`: Profile a research institution. Read a research institution's public identity, related institutions, confirmed affiliated papers and published affiliation spellings. (steps: papers.institutions.search, papers.institution.get, papers.search-filtered, papers.affiliations.search)
- `research.followup`: Research a topic across several questions. A multi-question research thread: your questions asked one after another in one remembered conversation, then a single write-up in which every finding names the answers and sources it rests on. (steps: web.answers-thread, llm.complete)
- `research.record.enrich`: Research fields for one record. Research the named fields for one record at an adequate bounded depth, keeping only sourced scalar facts. (steps: research.tier.suggest, research.fields.extract)
- `research.jobs.review`: Review your research runs. Review owned research runs, batches and question watches, and approve deletion of confirmed old terminal runs. (needs approval; steps: research.run.list, research.run.get, research.run.events, research.group.status, research.monitor.list, research.memory.search, research.run.delete)
- `literature.review`: Run a quick literature review. A bounded academic search with topic-checked records and sentences grounded in their own abstracts or checked pages. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, web.read, llm.complete)
- `paper.summarize`: Summarise a research paper. Summarise a verified paper from its own abstract, stored summary and checked public copies, with exact excerpt pointers. (steps: papers.get, papers.summary.get, papers.open-access.get, papers.locations.search, papers.location.get, web.read, document.parse, llm.complete)
- `paper.claim.support`: Test a claim against published papers. Find published passages and judge support and contradiction separately, retaining the exact passage and section. (steps: papers.snippets.search, papers.get, text.judge)
- `references.verify`: Verify a bibliography. Check each bibliography entry against a matching public record and report explicit differences without treating an outage as absence. (steps: papers.title.match, papers.search-filtered, papers.get, papers.venue.get)
- `research.watch`: Watch a research question over time. Reuse one standing question watch, adjust its cadence or create a missing managed watch, and read material answer changes. (steps: research.monitor.list, kv.entry.get, kv.entry.set, research.monitor.create, research.monitor.update, web.monitor.events)
- `topic.research.deep`: Write a deep research report. A long research report on a question, written only from cited pages that were re-read and checked. (steps: web.research, web.read, llm.complete)
