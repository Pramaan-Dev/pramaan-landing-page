# Missions: Gen AI

Make images, video, voice and music, and call language models.

- `memory.remember`: Add notes to memory. Notes or pages added to a subject's memory, one document per note under a stable id, with the store's own answer for each. A note that looks like it holds a secret is not stored. (steps: memory.document.add, memory.document.get)
- `video.motion.from-images`: Animate images into a video. Stills animated into clips, extended and joined into one sequence. (steps: video.effect-templates.list, video.effects.generate, video.animate, video.extend, video.merge)
- `memory.ask`: Ask what has been remembered. A question answered from what has been remembered about a subject, sentence by sentence, each sentence citing the remembered items it is taken from; says plainly when nothing remembered answers it. (steps: memory.search, memory.profile.get, llm.complete)
- `image.style.kit`: Build a house image style. A saved house style built from reference pictures, then used to render a batch of new images in that style, each read by an image check. (steps: image.style-presets.list, image.style.create, image.style.render, image.inspect)
- `model.print.check`: Check a 3D model for printing. A 3D model checked for printing and rigging, with a readable list of fixes. (steps: model3d.print-analyze, model3d.rig-check, model3d.animations.list)
- `image.moderate`: Check an image is safe. Report recognised image safety likelihood flags and an optional exact color comparison with the brand palette. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, brand.styleguide, apps.action.execute)
- `content.originality.check`: Check text for AI writing and copying. Whether a text reads machine-written, and whether its distinctive sentences already exist on other pages, each copy verified against the page's own text. (steps: text.ai.detect, web.search, web.read)
- `content.plagiarism.check`: Check text for plagiarism. Report the checker's matched text share and cited sources, with optional machine-written and readability estimates. (steps: web.read, text.plagiarism.check, text.plagiarism.check-long, text.ai.detect, text.readability.check)
- `voice.clone.setup`: Clone a voice. A cloned voice made from consented recordings, with its settings read and tuned. (steps: media.consent.get, audio.voice.clone, audio.voice.get, audio.voice-settings.update)
- `model.compare`: Compare AI models on your prompts. Identical prompt cases compared across quality tiers with exact-answer scores and token usage. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, llm.complete)
- `memory.correct`: Correct a remembered fact. Find and read an exact owned fact, preserve its old text and history, then correct it without approval or approve its deletion. (needs approval; steps: memory.items.search, memory.item.get, memory.item.history, memory.history.list, memory.item.update, memory.item.delete)
- `voice.design.setup`: Design a new voice. A new voice designed from a description: previews made, one kept and tuned. (steps: audio.voice-catalog.list, audio.voice.design, audio.voice.create, audio.voice-settings.update)
- `support.answer.draft`: Draft a support answer from your documents. A drafted support reply answered from the builder's own stored documents, each sentence checked against the passage it cites, saved as a draft in the inbox. Nothing is sent. (steps: email.thread.get, vector.index.get, text.embed, vector.vectors.query, text.rerank, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `ticket.answer.draft`: Draft an answer to a support ticket. A support reply grounded sentence by sentence in the caller's scoped documents, optionally saved as an approved private note. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, vector.index.get, text.embed, vector.vectors.query, text.rerank, llm.complete)
- `audio.dub`: Dub a recording into another language. A recording spoken again in another language: transcribed, translated, and spoken as generated audio files returned in order. (steps: audio.transcribe, text.translate, audio.speech)
- `video.dub`: Dub a video into another language. A video spoken again in another language in the speakers' own voices, followed until done, with an optional lip match. (steps: media.consent.get, video.translation.languages.list, media.dubbing.create, media.dubbing.get, video.lipsync.create, video.lipsync.get, video.lipsync.download)
- `video.enhance`: Enlarge a video. Enlarge a video while preserving its source detail. (steps: video.upscale)
- `memory.export`: Export a subject's memories. Create a structured copy of a subject's private memories, checked against owned source facts. (steps: memory.export.create, memory.export.get, memory.items.list, memory.summary.get, memory.filters.list)
- `rows.llm.fill`: Fill spreadsheet rows with AI. One instruction applied to up to 16 rows at a time, each reply checked against the shape you asked for. (steps: llm.tokens.count, llm.complete-bulk)
- `recording.quote.find`: Find a phrase in a recording. Every place a word or phrase is spoken in a recording, with time and the sentence around it. (steps: audio.transcription.create)
- `model.find`: Find an AI model for a job. A public model for a job (image, audio, text), with its input guide, versions and examples. (steps: model.catalog.search, model.catalog.list, model.collections.list, model.collection.get, model.catalog.get, model.documentation.get, model.examples.list, model.versions.list, model.version.get)
- `entities.mentioned`: Find people and places named in text. The people, organisations and places a text names, merged where spellings differ. (steps: text.entities.extract, text.fuzzy.compare)
- `image.generate-checked`: Generate images from a rough idea. Images from rough prompts: expanded, rendered with explicit controls, and rejected when a check finds unsafe content, missing required text or low quality. (steps: image.prompt.expand, image.generate-configured, image.generate-bulk, image.describe, image.inspect)
- `image.set.generate`: Generate images in your brand style. Images in a brand's own colours and style: the brand's visual identity is read from its site, a prompt per image is written and checked against it, and each prompt is generated into an image. (steps: brand.styleguide, llm.complete, image.generate)
- `texts.cluster`: Group similar texts. Group submitted texts by vector similarity, using an original member as each group's name. (steps: text.embed)
- `content.scan.log`: Keep a log of text scans. Check and retain labelled texts with independent detection reports and dated scan history. (needs approval; steps: text.scans.list, text.scan.create, text.scan.get)
- `image.describe-labels`: Label and describe an image. Return structured image labels and optional detected text, with a checked caption from those labels. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, llm.complete)
- `memory.learn.conversation`: Learn from a conversation. Preserve old conversation memory, store exact messages, extract facts and list their confirmed references for checking. (steps: memory.items.list, memory.add, memory.extraction.create, memory.events.list, memory.event.get)
- `talking-photo.make`: Make a photo talk. A portrait photo made to speak supplied text or audio, only when consent for that face is on record. (steps: media.consent.get, audio.speech, video.talking-photo.generate)
- `video.product.ad`: Make a product video ad. A product advert and a presenter-style product video from product images, with the concept checked against the product information. (steps: llm.complete, video.product-ad.generate, video.product-ugc.generate, video.reframe)
- `video.short.make`: Make a short video from a brief. A short video from a brief: script written and checked, scenes generated and joined, with narration, a soundtrack and subtitles as separate files. (steps: llm.complete, video.multi-shot.generate, video.merge, audio.speech, audio.soundtrack.generate, audio.subtitles.create)
- `clip.generate`: Make a video clip with narration. A short video clip and a separate narration file from one script, not a mixed clip: the script becomes one checked visual prompt for the video, and the script itself is spoken as the narration. (steps: llm.complete, video.generate, audio.speech)
- `video.formats.make`: Make a video in every format. One video delivered in the shapes each channel needs, with a thumbnail for each and an optional enlarged copy. (steps: video.reframe, video.thumbnail.get, video.frame.extract, video.upscale)
- `avatar.video.generate`: Make a video with a stock avatar. Submit an exact caller-written script with a public preset avatar and public voice, and return a generated video only when its matching job confirms completion. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `avatar.presenter.video`: Make a video with your avatar. A video of an avatar built from a consented person, speaking the supplied script. (steps: media.consent.get, video.avatar.create, video.avatar.get, video.avatar.look.get, audio.voice-catalog.list, video.avatar.render, video.render.get)
- `explainer.illustrated`: Make an illustrated explainer. A short explainer with text and generated images in order, rendered to HTML. (steps: llm.complete-illustrated, text.markdown.render, text.readability.check)
- `image.campaign.generate`: Make campaign images from a product photo. Four campaign images from a product picture and a brief, in the brand's own look, each read by an image check before it is returned. (steps: brand.styleguide, llm.complete, image.product-campaign.generate, image.marketing-stock.generate, image.inspect)
- `image.product.shots`: Make clean product photos. A clean product photo from one uploaded image: background removed or replaced, shadow added, framing fixed, optionally relit and enlarged, then checked for quality. (steps: image.background.remove, image.background.replace, image.shadow.add, image.layout.adjust, image.relight, image.upscale, image.inspect)
- `audio.caption.sync`: Make subtitles from a script and recording. Timed subtitles from a known script and its recording, written as SRT or VTT. (steps: audio.align, audio.subtitles.create)
- `video.subtitles.make`: Make video subtitles. Subtitle files in the original language and a chosen one, from a video link or upload, with the caption timing checked. (steps: media.video.formats.list, media.video.download, video.dialogue.transcribe, audio.subtitles.create, text.translate)
- `model.pick`: Pick the cheapest AI model that works. Compare quality tiers against your exact-answer cases and show available catalogue limits. (steps: llm.models.list, llm.tokens.count, llm.complete)
- `copy.edit`: Polish a draft's spelling and tone. A draft with spelling and grammar fixed, rewritten in the tone you name, and scored for readability and quality. (steps: text.grammar.check, text.rewrite, text.readability.check, text.quality.check)
- `recording.redact`: Redact a recording. A recording and its transcript with chosen personal details removed, and a count of what was removed. (steps: audio.redaction.create)
- `memory.pages.refresh`: Refresh remembered web pages. Re-read selected stored web pages and replace only complete, changed content, returning its old value. (steps: memory.documents.list, memory.document.get, web.read, memory.document.update)
- `music.remix`: Remix a track. A track split into stems, then transformed, extended or part-replaced from a prompt. (steps: audio.stems.separate, audio.music.transform, audio.music.extend, audio.music.inpaint)
- `passages.dedupe`: Remove near-duplicate passages. Find near-duplicate passages within selected ids in a cosine index, remove only after approval, and confirm absence. (needs approval; steps: vector.index.get, vector.vectors.get, vector.vectors.query-by-id, vector.vectors.delete)
- `image.object.remove`: Remove objects from a picture. Unwanted objects or text removed from a picture: each named object is found among the picture's detected objects, masked and erased, and the result is checked. (steps: image.objects.detect, image.segment, image.erase, image.text.remove, image.inspect)
- `text.redact-checked`: Remove personal data from text. Personal data removed from a text, then re-scanned to show nothing of the listed kinds is left. (steps: text.redact, text.entities.extract)
- `video.template.batch`: Render a video template many times. One video template rendered many times with different variables, each render followed until done. (steps: video.template.create, video.template.variables.update, video.avatar.render, video.render.get, video.thumbnail.get)
- `image.photo.restore`: Restore an old photo. An old or damaged photo repaired, optionally de-blurred, colourised, enlarged and compressed, with the original kept beside every stage's image. (steps: image.restore, image.deblur, image.colorize, image.upscale, image.compress)
- `memory.review`: Review what's in memory. The documents stored in one subject's memory, listed by title, summary and state (two pages), with the remembered profile and, on request, the namespace's own counts. Read-only. Learned memories and the documents' content are not listed. (steps: memory.documents.list, memory.namespaces.list, memory.profile.get)
- `ticket.triage`: Route support tickets. Classify explicit support tickets into fixed caller routes, hold caution cases, and optionally apply approved tags and routing. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, llm.complete)
- `model.run-job`: Run an AI model once. One public model run with validated inputs, read back until done, with the output copied to your files. (needs approval; steps: model.catalog.get, model.version.get, model.run, model.prediction.get, model.predictions.list)
- `model.run-batch`: Run up to 100 inputs through a model. Up to 100 inputs through one pinned model as a job, a row per input and a spend ceiling. (needs approval; steps: model.version.get, model.run-bulk, model.prediction.get)
- `content.scans.save`: Scan many texts and save the reports. Texts checked in bulk and each report kept with a label, so the same text can be compared across dates. (needs approval; steps: text.content.check-bulk, text.scans.list, text.scan.get, text.scan.create-bulk)
