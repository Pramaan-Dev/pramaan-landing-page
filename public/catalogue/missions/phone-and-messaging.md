# Missions: Phone & messaging

Calls, AI phone agents, SMS, WhatsApp, chat and one-time codes.

- `imessage.group.announce`: Announce to an iMessage group. Check every participant, then approve creating a group and sending its first message exactly once. (needs approval; steps: imessage.contact.check, messaging.opt-out.check, imessage.group.create)
- `voice.agent.build`: Build a voice agent. Create ready knowledge and named call actions, configure a voice agent, then approve one test call to the builder and read it back. (needs approval; steps: voice.knowledge-base.create, voice.knowledge-base.get, voice.tool.create, voice.assistant.create, voice.call.start-agent, voice.call.get)
- `phone.number.setup`: Buy and set up a phone number. Find and purchase a number after recurring-charge approval, report identity needs and configure an active number for voice. (needs approval; steps: phone.numbers.search, phone.number.availability.check, phone.number.purchase, phone.number.identity.requirements.get, phone.number.purchase-status, phone.number.voice.configure)
- `voice.call.bulk-agent`: Call a list with a voice agent. Approve calls by one speaking configuration to a checked recipient list and return each call outcome. (needs approval; steps: messaging.opt-out.check, voice.call.start-agent-bulk, voice.call.get)
- `chat.catchup`: Catch up on a channel. What happened in a channel since a time: the messages read, a summary, and the questions left open. (steps: chat.messages.list, chat.channel.get, chat.channel.unread-counts.get, llm.complete)
- `otp.phone.check`: Check a phone and send a code. Check a phone's type, send a code after approval, then check or resend its challenge on a later call. (needs approval; steps: phone.number.parse, otp.challenge.get, otp.send, otp.resend, otp.verify)
- `sms.readiness.check`: Check you can text US numbers. Whether this account can text US numbers yet: the texting registration, the US numbers on the account, and what is missing. (steps: sms.registration.status, phone.numbers.list)
- `sender.health.report`: Check your messaging sender health. Read selected messaging and calling health sources, report their states and list what needs attention. (steps: whatsapp.sender.quality.get, whatsapp.account.health.get, phone.number.health.get, sms.campaign.get, sms.brand.get)
- `phone.list.clean`: Clean a list of phone numbers. Check phone-number validity and carrier facts, marking duplicates and unusable rows. (steps: people.phone.verify, people.phone.carrier)
- `outreach.list.hygiene`: Clean an outreach contact list. Find invalid and duplicate addresses and unsubscribed people in a contact list, and block the bad ones one update at a time. (steps: outreach.contacts.list, people.email.verify, outreach.contact.update)
- `sms.registration.complete`: Complete your SMS registration. Submit an approved business/campaign or toll-free filing, or check its current review state. (needs approval; steps: sms.registration.status, sms.registration.submit, sms.registration-toll-free.submit, sms.registration-toll-free.status)
- `voice.sip.connect`: Connect a phone line to calling. Create an external call trunk, attach an existing number and place one approved test call. (needs approval; steps: phone.number.get, voice.sip-trunk.create, phone.number.sip-trunk.attach, voice.call.start)
- `broadcast.draft`: Draft a broadcast message. A broadcast written from the builder's brief, checked sentence by sentence against it, and loaded with their own subscribed contacts, as a draft. Nothing is sent. (steps: outreach.contacts.list, llm.complete, outreach.broadcast.create)
- `sms.delivery.audit`: Find out why texts failed. Audit a page of failed text deliveries, count source causes and prepare reviewed resend candidates without sending. (steps: sms.delivery-reports.list, sms.batches.list, sms.batch.get, sms.message.validate, llm.complete)
- `outreach.contacts.import`: Import leads as contacts. Leads added as contacts to your messaging inbox, each address checked first, consent recorded, duplicates and invalid addresses held back. (steps: people.email.verify, outreach.contacts.list, outreach.contact.create)
- `sms.template.launch`: Launch a text message template. Draft and store a static text template, simulate it, then approve activation without sending a message. (needs approval; steps: llm.complete, sms.template.create, sms.template-version.create, sms.send-preview, sms.template-version.activate)
- `voice.browser-call.run`: Make a phone call from the browser. Open a page phone session, approve one call, read its events across follow-up runs and explicitly close it. (needs approval; steps: voice.browser-session.create, voice.browser-call.start, voice.browser-session.events, voice.browser-session.close)
- `outreach.optout.apply`: Mark someone as opted out. One person marked opted out in your messaging inbox and removed from every sequence the account lists. The opt-out is written first, with no approval; each removal asks for one. (needs approval; steps: outreach.contacts.list, outreach.sequences.list, outreach.contact.update, outreach.sequence.unenroll)
- `chat.moderate`: Moderate a chat channel. Sort a channel's latest messages into abuse, spam and questions, then remove flagged messages and optionally freeze the channel after approval. (needs approval; steps: chat.messages.list, chat.channel.moderators.list, chat.channel.get, text.classify, chat.message.delete, chat.channel.freeze)
- `chat.room.open`: Open a chat channel for people. Create a channel for named people, making any missing profiles, and send a welcome message, after approval. (needs approval; steps: chat.user.get, chat.user.create, chat.channel.members.list, chat.channel.create, chat.message.send)
- `whatsapp.template.pick`: Pick a WhatsApp message template. The right approved messaging template for a message (or the one you name), with your own variable values, sent to one person only when asked and approved. (needs approval; steps: whatsapp.templates.list, llm.complete, whatsapp.template.send)
- `voice.agent.call`: Place a call with your voice agent. Place one call through your own speaking configuration after approval and return its outcome and grounded speech summary. (needs approval; steps: voice.numbers.list, voice.assistants.list, voice.squads.list, voice.call.start-agent, phone.numbers.list, voice.call.start-configured, llm.complete)
- `chat.announce.post`: Post an announcement to a channel. Draft a notice from the facts you give and post it to a team or community channel after approval. (needs approval; steps: llm.complete, chat.message.post)
- `call.insights`: Pull insights from a sales call. A caller-supplied sales recording's cited objections, next steps and risks, optionally stored as an approved CRM note. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, audio.transcription.create, llm.complete)
- `message.multichannel.fallback`: Reach someone across several channels. Try configured consented channels in priority order, falling back only after confirmed no-send or failed SMS delivery. (needs approval; steps: messaging.opt-out.check, whatsapp.message.send, rcs.message.send, sms.send, sms.status, email.send)
- `notify.device.register`: Register a phone for notifications. Register a push device, approve an exact test notification and read its delivery state once. (needs approval; steps: notify.device.create, notify.push.send, notify.message.get)
- `sms.conversation.reply`: Reply to a text message. Read an inbound text, check a drafted reply, and send that exact text only after approval. (needs approval; steps: sms.inbound-messages.list, sms.inbound-message.get, llm.complete, sms.send, sms.status)
- `imessage.conversation.reply`: Reply to an iMessage. Read an exact owned inbound message, check contact reachability, draft from caller notes and ask approval before replying. (needs approval; steps: imessage.contact.check, imessage.messages.list, llm.complete, imessage.message.reply, imessage.message.status)
- `phone.number.retire`: Retire a phone number. Check an owned number and account call activity, move its routing, then approve permanent release. (needs approval; steps: phone.number.get, voice.calls.list, phone.number.routing.update, phone.number.disconnection.check, phone.number.release)
- `call.review`: Review a call against your checklist. A sales or support call checked against a list of criteria about what was said or done: each met only with words quoted from the call, with its timestamp. (steps: audio.transcription.create, llm.complete)
- `voice.call.review`: Review a recorded call. Read a finished call recording, transcribe its speech and return a grounded summary and mentioned actions. (steps: voice.call.get-details, voice.call.recording.get, audio.transcription.create, llm.complete)
- `whatsapp.user.block-review`: Review blocked WhatsApp contacts. List blocked contacts and unblock only listed numbers the caller explicitly chose, preserving the original state. (steps: whatsapp.blocked-users.list, whatsapp.user.unblock)
- `outreach.automation.review`: Review your running outreach automations. Every running automation in one report: sequences, workflows and comment-to-message rules with their enrolment and run counts. (steps: outreach.sequences.list, outreach.workflows.list, outreach.comment-automations.list)
- `sms.delivery-campaign.run`: Run a paced text campaign. Start an approved paced text campaign or monitor one, pause on failures, and confirm approved completion. (needs approval; steps: messaging.opt-out.check, sms.delivery-campaign.create, sms.delivery-campaign.get, sms.delivery-campaign.messages.list, sms.delivery-campaign.pause, sms.delivery-campaign.resume, sms.delivery-campaign.finish)
- `voice.campaign.run`: Run a voice call campaign. Launch a voice campaign after opt-out checks and approval, or read, pause or complete one by its private reference. (needs approval; steps: messaging.opt-out.check, voice.campaign.get, voice.campaign.create, voice.campaign.pause, voice.campaign.finish, voice.campaign.calls.list)
- `broadcast.send`: Send a broadcast to subscribers. Draft a broadcast to subscribed contacts, show the recipient count, the opted-out removed and the message, and send it after approval. (needs approval; steps: outreach.contacts.list, outreach.broadcast.create, outreach.broadcast.send)
- `sms.campaign.send`: Send a bulk text campaign. One message sent to one person on the best channel your inputs allow (an approved messaging template, a rich message, a plain text, or email), with one delivery check afterwards. - `notify.digest.send`: Send a notification digest. Read unread events, arrange whole excerpts into one digest and approve its delivery on an explicitly enabled preferred channel. (needs approval; steps: notify.subscriber.get, notify.preferences.get, notify.inbox.list, llm.complete, notify.message.send)
