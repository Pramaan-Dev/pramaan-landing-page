# Missions: Advertising

Your ad accounts, competitors' ads, audiences and creator deals.

- `tag.container.audit`: Audit your tag manager. Read one container workspace's tags, triggers and variables, and name paused tags, tags that never fire, and unused triggers and variables. (steps: tagmanager.tags.list, tagmanager.triggers.list, tagmanager.variables.list)
- `ads.budget.pacing`: Check if campaigns are on budget. Daily campaign spend against its current daily budget, with currency and reporting coverage checked. (steps: ads.accounts.list, ads.campaigns.list, ads.insights.get)
- `ads.destination.check`: Check where your ads send people. Where each live ad sends people: the page reads, is not flagged unsafe and matches the ad's promise. (steps: ads.ads.list, web.read, security.url.check, web.screenshot, llm.complete)
- `ads.account.health`: Check your ads account for problems. One account check: paused or rejected ads, ad sets with no ads, tiny audiences and campaigns with no budget. (steps: ads.campaigns.list, ads.ad-sets.list, ads.ads.list, ads.ad.review.get, ads.audiences.list)
- `ads.competitor.compare`: Compare your ads with a competitor's. The builder's ads against a competitor's public ads: what each side runs, and the differences. (steps: ads.ads.list, ads.library.search, llm.complete)
- `ads.account.connect`: Connect your ads account. An ads account connected by sign-in link or dashboard key and proven by reading its ad accounts back. (steps: ads.account.connect.start, ads.account.connect.credentials, ads.accounts.list)
- `ads.creative.brief`: Draft new ad copy ideas. New ad copy ideas drafted from the builder's best performing ads and competitors' public ads, checked against the builder's own claims. Drafts only. (steps: ads.ads.list, ads.insights.get, ads.library.search, llm.complete)
- `ads.budget.estimate`: Estimate an ad budget for keywords. Keyword traffic at a chosen bid with separate historical readings and audience ranges, never a promise. (steps: google.keywords.ad-traffic, bing.audience.estimate, google.keywords.volume, bing.keywords.volume, bing.keywords.performance)
- `ads.waste.find`: Find ads that spent without results. Ads, ad sets and campaigns that spent money and produced no conversions, with the spend in each currency. (steps: ads.campaigns.list, ads.ad-sets.list, ads.ads.list, ads.insights.get)
- `sponsorship.scan`: Find paid promotion in a channel's videos. Which of a channel's recent videos carry paid promotion, with the source's detection text. (steps: youtube.channel.resolve, youtube.channel.videos, youtube.video.sponsors)
- `ads.review.check`: Find your rejected or flagged ads. Which of the builder's ads review rejected or flagged, with the platform's own reasons for each. (steps: ads.ads.list, ads.ad.review.get)
- `ads.audiences.report`: List your ad audiences and their sizes. The custom and lookalike audiences in the builder's ad accounts, with the sizes the platforms report. (steps: ads.accounts.list, ads.audiences.list)
- `ad.variants.make`: Make variations of an ad. One ad turned into a set: new sizes, translated copy and variations on one chosen axis, each read by an image check. (steps: llm.complete, image.ad.resize, image.ad.localize, image.ad.vary, image.inspect)
- `audience.keys.prepare`: Prepare customer lists for ad audiences. Hashed email and optional mobile advertising keys for the builder's own customers, without uploading them. (steps: people.hashes.find, people.adid.find)
- `ads.social.snapshot`: Read the message of social ads. Supplied social ads with their creative and a sourced reading of each message. (steps: linkedin.ad.detail, tiktok.ad.detail, llm.complete)
- `ads.performance.report`: Report on ad spend and results. Spend and results by campaign across the builder's ad accounts, with observations on what the numbers show. Money is never added across currencies, and nothing here is advice. (steps: ads.accounts.list, ads.campaigns.list, ads.insights.get, ads.ad-sets.list, ads.ads.list, llm.complete)
- `ad.intel`: Research ads from a public ad library. The ads in a public ad archive, grouped into themes: one advertiser's ads when an advertiser is named, otherwise ads matching a keyword. (steps: ads.library.search, llm.complete)
- `tiktok.ads.research`: Research TikTok ads. Research caller-supplied ad-library ids whose titles match a term, with engagement and grounded themes. (steps: tiktok.ad.detail, llm.complete)
- `ads.market.scan`: Scan up to 100 advertisers for live ads. Up to 100 advertisers or domains checked for active library ads in one job, as a table of ad counts and sample creatives. (steps: ads.library.search-bulk, ads.ad.detail-bulk)
- `ads.competitor.footprint`: See everything a competitor advertises. Everything a competitor advertises: library ads on three networks, search ad copy, keywords and landing pages, in one report. (steps: google.ads.advertisers.search, ads.library.search, ads.ad.detail, google.domain.paid-keywords, google.domain.paid-pages, google.domain.ad-history, llm.complete)
- `ads.creative.fatigue`: Spot ads that are wearing out. Ads whose clicks per impression fell over recent days while spend held, listed with the daily numbers. (steps: ads.ads.list, ads.insights.get)
