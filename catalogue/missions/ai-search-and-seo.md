# Missions: AI search and SEO

Rankings, keywords, backlinks, and how AI answer engines see your brand.

- `links.audit`: Audit a domain's backlinks. A domain's backlinks: totals, the newest links and their anchor texts, with risky linking sites marked. (steps: web.backlinks.summary, web.backlinks.list, web.anchors.list, security.url.check)
- `content.audit`: Audit a page for search. One page judged for search: its own text, whether the target keywords occur in it, its link authority and a written assessment. (steps: web.read, web.url.metrics, google.keywords.volume, text.ai.detect, llm.complete)
- `site.metadata.audit`: Audit titles and descriptions across a site. Title, description, canonical and social-card gaps across a site's pages, listed page by page. (steps: web.map, web.page.metadata, web.page.audit)
- `blog.audit`: Audit your latest blog posts. Check the latest posts of a connected blog for missing fields, drafts left unpublished and broken or unsafe links. (steps: blog.sites.list, blog.posts.list, blog.post.get, web.read, security.url.check)
- `local.listing.audit`: Audit your local business listing. Is one of the builder's business listings complete, and is it consistent with its own website and its public map listing. (steps: local.locations.list, local.location.get, web.read, google.serp.maps, local.menu.get, llm.complete)
- `local.seo.audit`: Audit your local search rankings. A connected business's positions in local results and neighbouring listings, with checked observations from listings and reviews. (steps: local.location.get, google.serp.local, google.serp.maps, local.reviews.list, llm.complete)
- `seo.audit`: Check a company's SEO. A domain's search standing: link authority and backlink totals by default; on request keyword volumes (give keywords), where it ranks and which sites rank ahead of it; written priorities unless turned off. (steps: web.url.metrics, web.backlinks.summary, google.keywords.ideas, google.keywords.volume, google.serp.organic, web.map, web.read, llm.complete)
- `aeo.overview.check`: Check if Google's AI overview cites you. For a keyword list, whether Google shows an AI overview, which sources it cites and whether the builder's site is one. (steps: google.serp.ai-overview, google.serp.organic-bulk, aeo.pages.top)
- `trend.check`: Check if interest in a term is rising. Whether interest in a term is rising: search interest over time, its search volume, and when asked samples from social search. (steps: google.keywords.trends, google.keywords.volume, tiktok.search.videos, youtube.search.videos, x.search.posts)
- `ai.overview.citations`: Check Naver's AI overview for your domain. For a list of queries: the AI overview Naver shows, the sources it cites, and whether a given domain is cited or ranks in the web results. (steps: naver.serp.ai-overview, naver.serp.organic)
- `image.source.find`: Check where an image is used. Check caller-supplied candidate page text for an exact image address, with an explicit status for every page. (steps: web.read)
- `listing.presence.check`: Check your business is listed on maps. Whether one business is listed on Google, Bing Maps, DuckDuckGo Maps, Yelp, Tripadvisor and Trustpilot, and whether name, address, website and phone agree. (steps: google.business.profile, google.serp.maps, bing.maps.search, duckduckgo.maps.search, yelp.business.search, tripadvisor.search, trustpilot.businesses.search)
- `keyword.country.compare`: Compare keywords across countries. Compare up to five keywords over time and across countries, preserving each source's figures. (steps: web.keywords.trends, web.keywords.volume, web.keywords.volume-by-country)
- `seo.local.rank.compare`: Compare local rankings across cities. The same local search run in several cities or areas, showing which businesses are listed where and at what position. (steps: google.serp.locations.list, google.serp.local, google.serp.maps)
- `search.compare`: Compare one search on three engines. The same query on three search engines side by side: each engine's results kept separate, plus the pages two or more of them return. (steps: google.serp.organic, bing.serp.organic, yahoo.serp.organic)
- `search.regional.compare`: Compare search in Baidu, Naver and Yandex. The same query on Baidu, Naver, Yandex and Seznam side by side, with titles translated and each engine kept separate. (steps: baidu.serp.organic, text.translate, baidu.serp.news, naver.serp.organic, yandex.serp.organic, yandex.serp.images, yandex.serp.videos, seznam.serp.organic)
- `seo.traffic.compare`: Compare search traffic across sites. Estimated search traffic for a list of sites, now and month by month, ranked side by side. The two sources' figures are kept apart. (steps: google.domains.traffic, google.domains.traffic-history, google.domain.traffic-distribution)
- `links.profile.compare`: Compare your backlinks with competitors. Your link profile against competitors and against the most linked-to sites on the web. (steps: web.backlinks.counts, web.linking-domains.counts, web.url.metrics, web.backlinks.competitors, web.global.top-domains, web.global.top-pages)
- `aeo.competitor.share`: Compare your brand with rivals in AI answers. A brand against its competitors in AI answers: mentions, search volume and top cited domains per brand, side by side. (steps: aeo.mentions.summary, aeo.domains.top, aeo.mentions.history, aeo.brands.top)
- `blog.site.connect`: Connect your blog or store. A blog or store connected and proven by reading its blogs and latest posts back. (steps: blog.site.connect.start, blog.site.connect.token, blog.sites.list, blog.posts.list)
- `blog.post.draft`: Draft a blog post from a keyword. Write a post from a keyword and the pages that rank for it, check it against those pages, and save it as a draft or schedule it on the connected blog. (needs approval; steps: google.serp.organic, google.keywords.ideas, web.read, blog.sites.list, llm.complete, blog.post.create)
- `content.draft`: Draft an article from a brief. An article drafted from a structured brief, with source pages read for background and a machine-written estimate of the draft. (steps: web.search, web.read, llm.complete, text.ai.detect)
- `local.questions.answer.draft`: Draft answers to local business questions. Unanswered public business questions with draft answers grounded in the business's own page; nothing is posted. (steps: google.business.questions, web.read, llm.complete)
- `seo.bing.opportunity`: Find Bing keyword opportunities. Site keyword opportunities with separate search estimates, month history and advertising benchmarks. (steps: bing.audience.estimate, bing.keywords.for-site, bing.keywords.ideas, bing.keywords.volume, bing.keywords.volume-history, bing.keywords.performance, google.keywords.volume)
- `keyword.gap`: Find keywords rivals rank for, not you. Keyword terms that competitors rank for and the builder's site does not, from one seed term. (steps: google.keywords.ideas, google.keywords.volume, google.serp.organic)
- `aeo.citation.gap`: Find pages AI answers cite instead of yours. The pages and domains AI answer engines most cite for a topic, against the builder's own pages: which of them are among the most cited pages returned and which are not. (steps: aeo.pages.top, aeo.domains.top, aeo.mentions.search, web.map)
- `image.usage.find`: Find pages using your logo or images. Pages that show the builder's own logo or product image, with the ones that are not the builder's own flagged. Used for brand and product images only, never to find a person from a photo. (steps: google.serp.lens, google.serp.images, bing.serp.images, web.read)
- `seo.quickwins`: Find quick SEO wins. Existing positions 4 to 20 with separate volume readings, ranked by an explicit hypothetical one-position gain index. (steps: google.domain.ranked-keywords, google.keywords.volume, google.keywords.difficulty)
- `links.gap`: Find sites linking to rivals but not you. Sites linking to competitors that were not in the builder's backlink sample, with optional authority estimates. (steps: web.backlinks.list, web.url.metrics)
- `image.find.match`: Find web images for a description. Web images for a description, each with the page it is on and its size, read against the description by its title and against the size asked for. (steps: image.search)
- `keyword.seasonality`: Find when keywords peak. The months a keyword set peaks and dips, from monthly search volume and ad price history and, separately, an interest trend, with the sources kept apart. (steps: google.keywords.history, google.keywords.trends, google.keywords.volume, llm.complete)
- `seo.keyword.cluster`: Group keywords by intent. Group seed keyword ideas by recorded intent, keeping volume, difficulty and category readings separate. (steps: google.keywords.ideas, google.serp.autocomplete, google.keywords.intent, google.keywords.difficulty, google.keywords.categories, google.keywords.volume)
- `search.query.suggest`: Improve your search wording. Better search wording for a topic: spell-checked and completed queries, each with its estimated searches a month from one volume dataset. (steps: web.query-correct, web.query-suggest, web.keywords.volume)
- `category.demand.map`: Map what people search in a category. The most searched keywords in a country or category, with volume, cost and difficulty, grouped into demand themes. (steps: google.keywords.top, google.keywords.categories, google.keywords.for-category, google.keywords.difficulty, llm.complete)
- `brand.visibility`: Measure your brand in AI answers. How often a sample of AI answer engines' answers mentions a brand across a set of prompts, which sites they cite, and estimated aggregate mention metrics for its domain. (steps: aeo.mentions.summary, aeo.chatgpt.answer, aeo.perplexity.answer, aeo.google.answer, aeo.claude.answer, aeo.brave.answer, aeo.brands.top, aeo.domains.top, llm.complete)
- `content.brief`: Plan an article from search data. An article outline from what already ranks and what people search: the ranking pages, keyword volumes, and sections that rest on them. (steps: google.serp.organic, web.read, google.keywords.ideas, google.keywords.volume, llm.complete)
- `aeo.fix.plan`: Plan page fixes to appear in AI answers. For prompts where the brand is missing, the pages the answers cite are read and page changes are proposed from what they cover and the builder's page lacks. (steps: aeo.chatgpt.answer, aeo.perplexity.answer, web.read, aeo.pages.top, llm.complete)
- `seo.competitor.profile`: Profile a competitor's search footprint. One competitor's search footprint: overview figures, ranked keywords, ranking pages, subdomains, traffic by country and the domains the source lists as its search competitors. (steps: google.domain.overview, google.domain.ranked-keywords, google.domain.pages, google.domain.subdomains, google.domain.competitors, google.domain.traffic-by-country, llm.complete)
- `local.competitor.profile`: Profile a local competitor on Google. A competing business's public Google profile: details, questions and answers, posts, recent reviews and published contacts. (steps: google.business.profile, google.business.questions, google.maps.posts, google.maps.contacts, google.maps.reviews)
- `blog.post.ship`: Publish a blog post. Read a draft, check its links, publish it after approval, and read the post and its live address back. (needs approval; steps: blog.post.get, security.url.check, web.read, blog.post.publish, blog.post.unpublish)
- `links.opportunities`: Rank link opportunities from competitors. Sites that link to several competitors but not to you, ranked by how many competitors they link to. (steps: web.linking-domains.intersect, web.backlinks.intersect, web.backlinks.status, web.spam-scores.list)
- `blog.post.refresh`: Refresh an old blog post. Compare a post with what now ranks, propose edits as a diff, and apply them to the post only after approval. (needs approval; steps: blog.post.get, google.serp.organic, google.domain.ranked-keywords, web.read, llm.complete, blog.post.update)
- `links.anchor.report`: Report on anchor text pointing at your site. The anchor texts pointing at a site, compared with the anchors it uses to itself and to others. (steps: web.anchors.list, web.internal-anchors.list, web.outbound-anchors.list)
- `links.portfolio.report`: Report on backlinks for up to 1,000 domains. Link totals, gains, losses and spam scores for up to a thousand domains in one tracked job. (steps: web.backlinks.counts-bulk, web.linking-domains.counts-bulk, web.backlinks.new-lost-bulk, web.linking-domains.new-lost-bulk, web.spam-scores.list-bulk, web.pages.backlinks-summary-bulk, web.url.metrics-bulk, web.backlinks.index-stats)
- `keyword.research`: Research keywords for a topic. Keyword ideas for a seed term with search volume and cost, and when asked the interest trend and who ranks. (steps: google.keywords.ideas, google.keywords.volume, google.keywords.trends, google.serp.organic)
- `aeo.prompt.research`: Research what people ask AI tools. The questions people ask AI tools about a topic, with how often each is used and, when asked, related web searches. (steps: aeo.mentions.search, aeo.keywords.volume, google.keywords.ideas)
- `links.risk.screen`: Screen for harmful backlinks. The linking sites most likely to hurt a profile (spam score, shared networks), with a short list to review. (steps: web.linking-domains.list, web.spam-scores.list, web.linking-networks.list, security.url.check)
- `search.sweep`: Search a question from several angles. One question searched from several angles (plain, rewritten, deep, news, and filtered by date and domain), merged and de-duplicated by page address, each page with the rank every angle gave it. (steps: web.search, web.broad.search, web.deep.search, web.news.search, web.search-options)
- `aeo.citations.report`: See how often AI assistants cite your site. How often each AI assistant cites the builder's site and how many of its pages, with the most cited pages. (steps: aeo.citations.count, aeo.pages.top, aeo.mentions.search)
- `links.changes.report`: See new and lost backlinks since a date. New and lost links and linking domains since a date, with the trend in authority. (steps: web.backlinks.new-lost, web.linking-domains.new-lost, web.backlinks.changes, web.backlinks.history, web.linking-domains.history, web.domain-rating.history)
- `seo.paid.spy`: See what a competitor pays for in search. What a competitor pays for in search: keywords they bid on, cost per click, landing pages and the ad copy they ran over time. (steps: google.domain.paid-keywords, google.domain.paid-pages, google.domain.ad-history, google.serp.ads, google.keywords.ad-traffic)
- `aeo.web-surface.check`: See what ChatGPT and Copilot say about you. What a person sees when they ask ChatGPT, Copilot and Google's assistant a prompt, and whether the brand and its pages appear. (steps: aeo.chatgpt.web-answer, aeo.copilot.web-answer, aeo.google.web-answer, google.serp.ai-mode, llm.complete)
- `brand.serp.check`: See what searchers find for your brand. What a person sees when they search a brand name on three engines, and what in it is not the brand's own. (steps: google.serp.organic, google.serp.news, google.serp.images, google.serp.videos, google.serp.features, google.serp.ai-overview, google.serp.autocomplete, bing.serp.organic, duckduckgo.serp.organic)
- `seo.serp.displacement`: See who gained or lost a search result. Who held a search result before and who holds it now: an earlier dated result page against today's, with the sites that moved in or out. (steps: google.serp.organic, google.serp.history, google.keywords.competitors, web.url.metrics)
- `seo.category.leaders`: See who leads a category in search. Who leads a product or service category in search: domains ranked by category traffic between two dates and by the category's keywords, each source's rank and figures kept apart. (steps: google.keywords.for-category, google.domain.category-metrics, google.keywords.competitors, google.domain.categories)
- `seo.serp.snapshot`: Snapshot every result type for a search. One query across every result type at once (organic, ads, news, images, videos, shopping, local, answer boxes, generated answer, other engines), so you see who owns each slot. (steps: google.serp.organic, google.serp.ads, google.serp.news, google.serp.images, google.serp.videos, google.serp.shorts, google.serp.shopping, google.serp.local, google.serp.features, google.serp.ai-overview, bing.serp.organic, duckduckgo.serp.organic, google.serp.autocomplete)
- `seo.domain.trend`: Track a site's search standing over time. How one site's search standing moved over time: keywords by position band, ranking pages and search volume reached, with the biggest swings named from the dated figures. (steps: google.domain.history, google.domain.keywords-history, google.domain.ranking-pages-history, google.domain.search-volume-history, llm.complete)
- `links.moves.report`: Track backlink wins and losses against rivals. New and lost backlinks for a site and its competitors over a period, with authority history, ranked by who gained most. (steps: web.backlinks.new-lost, web.backlinks.changes, web.backlinks.history, web.domain-rating.history, web.backlinks.status)
- `brand.visibility.history`: Track brand mentions in AI answers by month. A brand's estimated mentions in AI answers month by month, with the total over the months returned. (steps: aeo.mentions.history, aeo.mentions.summary)
- `rank.check`: Track your keyword rankings. Where a domain ranks for a list of terms on the primary search engine, and when asked two more. (steps: google.serp.organic, bing.serp.organic, yahoo.serp.organic)
- `local.listing.update`: Update your local business listing. One of your business listings updated from its own website: the description and hours the site states (and its menu, when given) are copied out and checked against the page; only the fields you name are written, with the values before returned so a write can be undone. (steps: local.location.get, web.read, local.menu.get, llm.complete, local.location.update, local.menu.update)
- `local.listing.verify`: Verify your local business listing. Show verification choices, request a code after approval, or complete the same pending verification after approval. (needs approval; steps: local.location.get, local.verification.get, local.verification.options, local.verification.start, local.verification.complete)
- `links.broken.reclaim`: Win back broken backlinks. Backlinks flagged broken on the requested site with checked current-page redirect suggestions; no redirect is changed. (steps: web.backlinks.list, web.backlinks.top-pages, web.backlinks.pages-summary, web.map, web.read, llm.complete)
