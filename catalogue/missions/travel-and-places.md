# Missions: Travel & places

Maps, places, addresses, property, routes, weather and travel.

- `map.layer.build`: Build a private map layer. A private map layer of the builder's own locations with a custom style and a rendered picture. (steps: maps.datasets.list, maps.styles.list, maps.dataset.create, maps.dataset.feature.put, maps.style.create, maps.style.update, maps.image.render)
- `store.locator.build`: Build a store locator. Build a bounded list of owned public store locations, with checked coordinates and matched regular opening hours. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, local.locations.list, apps.action.execute, local.location.get)
- `trip.trace.analyze`: Check a recorded drive against speed limits. A recorded drive snapped to roads and checked against speed limits, with elevation. (steps: roads.path.snap, roads.speed-limits.get, geo.elevation.profile.get)
- `address.check`: Check and correct an address. A connected address check with explicit validation status, corrected source formatting and optional map coordinates. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `delivery.zone.check`: Check if an address is in your delivery zone. Whether an address or position is inside a saved zone, with distance from the edge. (steps: geo.address.geocode, geo.geofence-layers.list, geo.geofence.check)
- `road.conditions.brief`: Check road conditions on a route. A brief on a route or area: incidents, traffic speeds and weather alerts, ranked by effect. (steps: roads.incidents.list, roads.traffic.flow, weather.alerts.list, roads.incident.get, routes.compute)
- `event.weather.check`: Check weather for an event. Check event weather against explicit thresholds and the returned forecast's actual time coverage. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `drive.weather.risk`: Check weather risk along a drive. A planned drive scored for weather risk along the road at the time each leg is driven. (steps: routes.compute, weather.route-conditions.get, weather.alerts.list)
- `addresses.clean.batch`: Clean up a list of addresses. A messy address list parsed, corrected, deduplicated and given coordinates, with rejects listed. (needs approval; steps: geo.address.parse, geo.address.validate-detailed, geo.address.geocode-detailed-bulk, geo.address.secondaries.count-bulk, geo.address.validation-feedback.send)
- `place.ratings.compare`: Compare a place's ratings on two maps. One place's rating and details on two maps compared, with photos copied and disagreements noted. (steps: places.search, places.details.get, places.photo.get, apple-maps.place.detail, apple-maps.place.ratings)
- `trip.options.compare`: Compare flights and hotels for a trip. Flights and hotels for dates and places, with hotel ratings and guest reviews beside price. Each source's figures are kept apart; no total is made. (steps: google.flights.search, google.hotels.search, google.hotel.detail, tripadvisor.search, tripadvisor.place.detail, tripadvisor.place.reviews)
- `commute.compare`: Compare ways to commute. A commute compared by car, transit and park-and-ride at chosen times, with the forecast and current traffic. (steps: routes.compute, routes.compute-intermodal, roads.traffic.flow, weather.forecast.hourly)
- `local.area.density`: Count local businesses by category. How many businesses of a kind are in an area and how they split by category, to size local competition. (steps: google.business.categories.list, google.business.categories-aggregate, google.business.listings.search)
- `area.density.report`: Count places of a kind in an area. How many places of a kind sit in an area, with rating, price and open-state spread, plus the nearby business list. (steps: places.area.insights, places.categories.list, places.search, local.businesses.search)
- `trip.plan.draft`: Draft a trip plan. A draft trip: flights, hotel, things to do, weather and a cost sketch, each part sourced. (steps: travel.flight-offers.search, travel.hotel-offers.search, places.search, weather.forecast.daily, llm.complete)
- `delivery.zone.define`: Draw a delivery zone. A delivery zone drawn from drive time or distance and saved as a private zone layer. (steps: geo.address.geocode, geo.isochrone.generate, geo.geofence-layer.create, geo.geofence-layer.get)
- `delivery.eta`: Estimate a delivery arrival time. Estimate arrival from a checked driving route and report destination forecast flags without inventing weather delay. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `solar.potential.estimate`: Estimate solar yield for a site. A rough solar yield for a site on a chosen day from irradiance, with climate and roof facts beside it. (steps: weather.solar.irradiance, weather.climate.normals, geo.property.lookup, geo.elevation.get)
- `ev.charger.nearby`: Find EV chargers nearby. Chargers near a point that fit a plug and power need, with live point status. (steps: ev.chargers.search, ev.charger.availability, ev.operators.list)
- `local.events.find`: Find events near a city. Events and places near a city: event search, the city's events page, named venue pages, and photo network locations, as one list by date. (steps: facebook.events.search, facebook.city.events, facebook.page.events, facebook.event.detail, instagram.cities.list, instagram.search.locations, instagram.location.detail)
- `flight.options.find`: Find flight options. Bookable flights for a trip with the fare rechecked, the expiry shown and seat layouts. Nothing is booked. (steps: travel.locations.search, travel.flight-offers.search, travel.flight-offer.get, travel.flight-offer.price, travel.flight-seatmaps.get)
- `home.shortlist`: Find homes for sale. Find homes offered for sale in a location. (steps: real-estate.search-homes-for-sale)
- `hotel.options.find`: Find hotel rooms and rates. Find room rates near trip coordinates, reprice a bounded selection and read available weather. (steps: travel.hotel-offers.search, travel.hotel-offer.price, weather.forecast.daily)
- `local.business.find`: Find local businesses. Local businesses for a query and place, with their own websites read. (steps: google.serp.maps, web.read, companies.enrich)
- `agent.find`: Find local property agents. Find local property professionals. (steps: real-estate.agents.search)
- `parking.find`: Find parking near a destination. Parking near a destination with live availability, tariffs for the stay and walking time. (steps: places.parking.search, places.parking.availability, places.parking-prices.get, routes.compute)
- `places.nearby.find`: Find places near an address. Find category-matched places near a checked postal address, with checked driving distance and time when requested. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, google.serp.maps)
- `best-time.to-visit`: Find the best time to visit a place. The best months to visit a place from climate normals, statistics and recent history. (steps: weather.climate.statistics, weather.history.daily)
- `transit.departures.next`: Find the next transit departures. The next departures from the nearest stations, with a walking-and-transit trip when a destination is given. (steps: transit.stations.search, transit.station.get, transit.departures.list, routes.compute)
- `location.brief`: Get a brief on a location. Recent weather, air readings and category-matched nearby places for a checked public postal address. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, google.serp.maps)
- `weather.brief`: Get a weather brief. A plain brief for a place and day: now, hourly, daily and rain timing, with alerts. (steps: weather.current.get, weather.forecast.hourly, weather.forecast.daily, weather.precipitation.forecast, weather.alerts.list, weather.summary.get)
- `air.quality.advice`: Get air quality advice for outdoors. Air quality now, next days and the recent past with plain outdoor-activity advice. (steps: weather.air-quality.current, weather.air-quality.forecast, weather.air-quality.history)
- `area.demographics.report`: Get census data for US addresses. Read census geography and precision for up to fifty US addresses. (steps: geo.address.census.lookup)
- `location.environment.brief`: Get weather and air readings for a place. Location- and time-checked weather and air measurements, with an explicit unavailable satellite view. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `map.image.make`: Make a map image. A static map picture of a route or a list of places, ready to put in a message. (steps: geo.address.geocode, routes.compute, maps.image.render)
- `ev.trip.plan`: Plan an electric-vehicle trip. An electric-vehicle route with its charging stops, live status at each stop and other chargers near any stop without a live reading. (steps: routes.compute-electric, ev.charger.availability, ev.chargers.search)
- `deliveries.plan`: Plan routes for up to 100 deliveries. Up to 100 deliveries split across vehicles in optimised routes, with unplaceable addresses listed. (steps: geo.address.geocode, geo.address.geocode-detailed-bulk, routes.tours.optimize, routes.compute)
- `day.route.plan`: Plan the best route for a day. A day's stops put in the best order with arrival times, travel legs and the forecast at each stop. (steps: geo.address.geocode, routes.stops.sequence, routes.compute, weather.route-point.get)
- `property.profile`: Profile a US property. Building facts for a US address: units, census area, businesses at the address and elevation. Owner fields stay off. (steps: geo.property.lookup, geo.address.census.lookup, geo.address.secondaries.count, geo.address.businesses.list, geo.elevation.get)
- `map.issue.report`: Report a map error. A map error checked against public map features and then reported to the map maker, once approved. (needs approval; steps: maps.features.query, maps.feedback.create, maps.feedback.get)
- `neighbourhood.report`: Report on a neighbourhood. A profile of an address's surroundings: census geography, nearby services, road congestion, air quality and schools named. (steps: geo.address.geocode, geo.address.census.lookup, places.search-nearby, roads.traffic.flow, weather.air-quality.current, schools.clean)
- `season.accumulation.report`: Report rain or heat totals for a place. Heat or rain totals since a date for a place, with the forecast ahead, for crop or pest timing. (steps: weather.accumulation.temperature, weather.accumulation.precipitation, weather.forecast.daily)
- `local.market.scan`: Scan who serves a local area. Who else serves an area for a kind of business: the listings two local searches return, merged, with ratings and review counts. Each business carries its position in every source that listed it; the combined order is the best (lowest) position in any source, ties broken by the number of sources listing it, then by source order. (steps: google.serp.maps, google.serp.local)
- `site.score`: Score a site for a new location. A candidate site measured: reachable area, competing places, parking, traffic and census geography, each number from its own source. (steps: geo.address.geocode, geo.isochrone.generate, places.area.insights, places.parking.search, roads.traffic.flow, geo.address.census.lookup)
- `weather.alert.notify`: Send weather alerts to people. Read the weather alerts at a place and, after approval, text and email people when one is serious enough. (needs approval; steps: weather.alerts.list, weather.alert.get, weather.fire-index.get, sms.send, email.send)
- `weather.event.verify`: Verify a weather event hit an address. Whether a weather event hit an address on a date, from recorded events, recorded history and a modelled timeline. (steps: geo.address.geocode, weather.events.search, weather.history.get, weather.timeline.get)
- `weather.thresholds.watch`: Watch a schedule against weather limits. A schedule or site checked against stated weather limits, each failing window given with the hour it starts. (steps: weather.route-conditions.get-bulk, weather.forecast.quarter-hourly, weather.forecast.three-hourly)
