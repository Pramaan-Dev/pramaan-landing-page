# Missions: Hiring & assessments

Jobs, pay, candidates and proctored tests.

- `interview.assess`: Assess communication in an interview. Text-based communication observations from caller-designated interview speech, each with checked quotes and transcript timestamps. (steps: audio.transcription.create, llm.complete)
- `job.posting.audit`: Audit your job ad. Audit your ad's clarity with a sourced rubric and separate advertised-pay datasets. (steps: job.posting.analyze, job.title.enrich, job.market.salary-distribution, job.postings.search, llm.complete)
- `job.salary.benchmark`: Benchmark pay for a job. Keep salary thresholds, history and advertised job ranges separate, with the observed posting sample size. (steps: job.market.salary-distribution, job.market.salary-history, job.postings.search)
- `assessment.exam.build`: Build and publish an exam. Create a draft exam, read back questions in order and publish only after confirmation. (needs approval; steps: assessment.exam.create, assessment.question.create, assessment.questions.list, assessment.exam.get, assessment.exam.publish)
- `job.posting.verify`: Check a job ad isn't a scam. Check an ad's warning signs against employer registration, domain facts, headcount and news; never guarantee genuineness. (steps: job.posting.analyze, companies.registry.search, companies.employment-registration.verify, companies.headcount.get, companies.news, security.domain.whois)
- `market.hiring.compare`: Compare hiring across places and roles. Compare employer-matched open role samples by function and place, with separate workforce histories and honest unknown seniority. (steps: job.postings.search-filtered, companies.jobs.search-bulk, companies.workforce)
- `salary.benchmark`: Compare salary sources. Salary sources side by side with their own units, samples and dates, without averaging sources. (steps: job.market.salary-distribution, job.market.salary-history, job.salaries.salary-data, job.crypto.salaries)
- `candidate.outreach.draft`: Draft a first message to a candidate. A checked first-message draft from public job history and your role; nothing is emailed. (steps: people.enrich, people.profile.details, job.posting.analyze, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `candidate.source`: Find candidates for a role. People whose current title matches a role at one employer, with checked profile identity and optional work email. (steps: people.search, linkedin.user.profile, people.email.find)
- `candidate.lookalikes.find`: Find candidates like your ideal hire. Similar professional profiles from job facts, with profile links only. (steps: people.lookalike.search)
- `job.match`: Match a resume to job titles. Employer-checked job titles ranked against checked professional resume text, with an optional connected-app ranking for comparison. (needs approval; steps: document.resume.parse, apps.connections.list, document.parse, companies.jobs.search, text.rerank, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `candidate.match`: Match resumes to job requirements. For each candidate's resume, every job requirement read as evidenced (with a verbatim quote), not evidenced or not checked; candidates ordered in code by the count of evidenced requirements. (steps: document.resume.parse, document.parse, llm.complete)
- `jobs.market.scan`: Scan open roles for a job title. The open roles for a title across two job sources: counts per company, location and skill computed in code, with the postings behind them. (steps: linkedin.jobs.search, google.jobs.search, linkedin.job.detail, llm.complete)
- `resume.screen`: Screen a resume against requirements. Quoted evidence from one resume for each job requirement the caller states, gathered for a human reviewer: each requirement reads evidenced, not_evidenced or not_checked. Nothing is added up or ordered, and a person makes the decision. (steps: document.resume.parse, llm.complete)
- `job.search.multi-board`: Search several job boards at once. Combine available job searches into one deduplicated source-linked list. (steps: job.postings.search, job.postings.search-filtered, companies.jobs.search)
- `job.skills.demand`: See which skills employers want. Count catalog skill and tool mentions in sampled role postings across two dated windows; return sample changes, not a market trend. (steps: job.postings.search-export, job.title.enrich, job.postings.tags.list, job.postings.occupation.taxonomy, job.postings.filters.resolve)
- `assessment.invite.send`: Send exam invites to candidates. Hold assessment credit with confirmation, then save candidate invitation emails as drafts. To email a link instead, run assessment.invitation.create, then email.send (own Gmail or Outlook by default, or sender: "pramaan"). (needs approval; steps: assessment.exams.list, assessment.invitations.list, assessment.invitation.create, email.draft.create)
- `candidate.pool.size`: Size your talent pool. Size a professional search with separate dataset totals, a masked sample and advertised pay thresholds. (steps: people.search-count, people.search-preview, job.market.salary-distribution)
- `job.market.snapshot`: Snapshot the job market. A sourced page of advertised categories, regions, employers and monthly pay, with separate index coverage. (steps: job.market.categories, job.market.regions, job.market.employers, job.market.salary-history, job.postings.dataset.stats, llm.complete)
- `job.titles.normalize`: Standardise job titles. Normalize free-text titles and keep exact catalog occupation matches and related skills. (steps: job.title.enrich, job.postings.roles.list, job.postings.titles.list, job.postings.occupation.taxonomy)
- `employer.verify`: Verify an employer. Employer registration facts, provident fund filing counts and a company-register name comparison. (steps: companies.employment-registration.verify, companies.registry.search, companies.workforce-filings.list, companies.tax-registration.verify, companies.name.match)
- `jobs.search.watch`: Watch a job search for new roles. Start an employer-only saved job search as a standing monthly rental; read new deliveries by reference. (steps: data.monitor.list, companies.jobs.monitor.create, job.postings.search)
- `linkedin.jobs.watch`: Watch LinkedIn for new matching jobs. A saved job search for a schedule: the professional network job posts that match and were posted inside the look-back window, newest first. (steps: linkedin.jobs.search, linkedin.job.detail)
- `assessment.invites.clean-up`: Withdraw unused exam invites. Review unused invitations and withdraw them after confirmation to release held credit. (needs approval; steps: assessment.invitations.list, assessment.invitation.get, assessment.invitation.revoke)
