# Missions: Crypto & DeFi

Tokens, wallets, chains, DeFi yields and on-chain data.

- `chain.question.answer`: Answer a question from blockchain data. Answer a bounded public-chain sample question with a compiled read-only query, checked and costed before execution. (steps: llm.complete, crypto.onchain.sql-estimate, crypto.onchain.sql)
- `exchange.price.compare`: Compare a crypto price across exchanges. Compare the same spot pair across requested exchanges with separate quotes and depth. (steps: crypto.exchange.ticker, crypto.exchange.order-book)
- `tokenized.stock.compare`: Compare a stock with its on-chain price. A listed share quote beside its associated on-chain market prices, with unit and timing limits. (steps: stocks.quote.live, stocks.company.onchain-markets)
- `borrow.rates.compare`: Compare crypto borrowing rates. Compare reported borrowing rates for an exact pool token name within the markets read. (steps: crypto.borrow.markets)
- `bridge.compare`: Compare crypto bridges. Compare bridge volume rankings with separate chain-wide daily token figures. (steps: crypto.bridges.list, crypto.bridges.ranking, crypto.bridges.day-stats)
- `venue.compare`: Compare crypto trading venues. Venue volume rankings within spot, decentralized and options categories, with aggregate derivatives context. (steps: crypto.exchanges.list, crypto.dexs.overview, crypto.derivatives.overview, crypto.options.overview)
- `staking.compare`: Compare staking rates and yields. Separate factual tables of liquid staking exchange rates and reported earning-pool yields. (steps: crypto.liquid-staking.rates, crypto.earn.pools)
- `crypto.brief`: Get a brief on a crypto token. An exact token's reported quote and 24-hour move, dated name-matching news snippets, and a checked descriptive brief. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, google.serp.news, llm.complete)
- `crypto.market.brief`: Get a crypto market snapshot. A factual market snapshot with market totals, sentiment index, futures, selected-asset liquidations and fund flows. (steps: crypto.market.global, crypto.fear-greed.get, crypto.futures.overview, crypto.liquidations.by-exchange, crypto.etf.flows, crypto.news.feed)
- `chain.market.report`: Report on a blockchain's market. Chain-matched value locked, stablecoin supply and dated bridge flows, with coverage gaps stated. (steps: crypto.chains.tvl, crypto.stablecoins.chains, crypto.bridges.volume-history)
- `wallet.report`: Report on a crypto wallet. Check one EVM wallet and report chain holdings, DeFi positions, value samples and a bounded activity feed without inferring its owner's identity. (steps: crypto.ens.resolve, crypto.address.validate, crypto.wallet.portfolio, crypto.wallet.defi-positions, crypto.wallet.net-worth, crypto.wallet.history)
- `defi.protocol.report`: Report on a DeFi protocol. Read a protocol profile, selected chain TVL, fees, revenue and flows, keeping source figures separate. (steps: crypto.protocol.detail, crypto.protocol.tvl-metrics, crypto.fees.protocol, crypto.protocol.financials, crypto.protocol.treasury, crypto.protocol.flows, crypto.hacks.list)
- `token.risk.report`: Report on a token's risks. Token holder shares, ticker-based unlocks and sales, protocol usage and mechanically listed observations. (steps: crypto.token.holders, crypto.token.resolve, crypto.token.unlocks, crypto.token.public-sale, crypto.token.protocols, crypto.hacks.list)
- `treasury.companies.report`: Report on companies' crypto holdings. Caller-named listed companies' crypto treasuries, transactions and share prices. (steps: crypto.treasury-companies.list, crypto.treasury-company.detail, stocks.company.profile, stocks.quote.live)
- `perp.positioning.report`: Report on perpetual futures positioning. Observed perpetual funding, long-to-short ratios, open interest and options for a token. (steps: crypto.perp.snapshot, crypto.perp.funding-rates, crypto.perp.long-short, crypto.options.market)
- `rwa.market.report`: Report on tokenised real-world assets. Tokenised assets by class, issuer and chain beside fund flow snapshots. (steps: crypto.rwa.list, crypto.rwa.chain, crypto.rwa.stats, crypto.etf.snapshot)
- `crypto.fund.diligence`: Research a crypto fund. A uniquely matched fund's profile, portfolio and reported tier, with ranking-page context. (steps: crypto.funds.search, crypto.funds.ranking, crypto.fund.profile, crypto.fund.portfolio)
- `hyperliquid.trader.review`: Review a Hyperliquid trader's wallet. A wallet's reported account on the Hyperliquid exchange: position measurements, orders, fills and closed-trade results. (steps: crypto.hyperliquid.account, crypto.hyperliquid.positions, crypto.hyperliquid.orders, crypto.hyperliquid.fills, crypto.hyperliquid.trades, crypto.hyperliquid.trades-summary)
- `airdrop.scout`: Scout crypto airdrops. Keyword-matching airdrops, reported tasks and dated upcoming events from bounded pages. (steps: crypto.airdrops.search, crypto.airdrops.activities, crypto.events.search)
- `wallet.counterparty.screen`: Screen a crypto wallet's history. Wallet age and sampled activity as facts, with unsupported ownership and sanctions links stated explicitly. (needs approval; steps: crypto.address.validate, crypto.wallet.age, crypto.wallet.transactions)
- `yield.opportunities.screen`: Screen crypto yield opportunities. Rank reported pool yields and flag possible hack-name links without inferring safety. (steps: crypto.yield.pools, crypto.earn.verified-pools, crypto.protocol.detail, crypto.hacks.list)
- `token.unlock.calendar`: See upcoming token unlocks. Upcoming next token unlocks in an explicit window, ranked by their reported share of circulating supply. (steps: crypto.tokens.emissions, crypto.market.rankings)
- `crypto.fundraising.digest`: Summarise crypto fundraising for a month. Dated raises in a chosen month-sized window, with matched project and investor profiles. (steps: crypto.raises.list, crypto.fundraising.search, crypto.project.detail, crypto.fund.profile, crypto.fund.portfolio)
- `listing.events.digest`: Summarise exchange listings and delistings. Exchange listings and delistings with related source events and headlines. (steps: crypto.listings.events, crypto.events.search, crypto.news.search)
- `token.flow.trace`: Trace where a token moves. Sample token transfers, top recipients, exchange swaps and hash-checked transaction details without inferring ownership. (steps: crypto.token.transfers, crypto.token.counterparties, crypto.token.dex-trades, crypto.token.holders, crypto.token.transfer-stats, crypto.transaction.detail)
- `stablecoin.peg.watch`: Watch stablecoin pegs. Sample stablecoin USD peg readings and dated supply changes, with chain totals kept separate. (steps: crypto.stablecoins.list, crypto.stablecoin.detail, crypto.stablecoins.chains)
- `crypto.signals.watchlist`: Watch which crypto projects get attention. Sampled project and token attention rankings, with optional explicitly selected indicators and keyword news. (steps: crypto.signals.projects, crypto.signals.token-of-day, crypto.market.trending, crypto.technical.indicator, crypto.onchain.indicator, crypto.project.ai-news)
