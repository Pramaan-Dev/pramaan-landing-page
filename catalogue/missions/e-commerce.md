# Missions: E-commerce

Products, prices, reviews and marketplaces, through to shipping and tracking.

- `catalog.price.align`: Align your prices with the market. Own catalog prices compared with the market, with only the named fields written back and the old values returned. (steps: commerce.products.list, commerce.prices.compare, commerce.product.upsert)
- `china.shop.audit`: Audit a shop on a Chinese marketplace. Store products and sales, the fetched page's best seller, its reviews and a checked summary. (steps: douyin-shop.shop.items, douyin-shop.item.detail, douyin-shop.item.comments, llm.complete)
- `tiktok.shop.store.audit`: Audit a TikTok Shop store. A TikTok Shop store from a share link: its id and address, its products, and the best sellers with their reviews. (steps: tiktok-shop.shop.resolve, tiktok-shop.shop.products, tiktok-shop.product.detail, tiktok-shop.product.reviews, llm.complete)
- `app.listing.audit`: Audit an app store listing. An app's listing on one or both stores audited: description, rating, price, update dates and the keywords it ranks for, against its top competitors. (steps: app-store.app.detail, app-store.app.keywords, app-store.app.competitors, google-play.app.detail, google-play.app.keywords, google-play.app.competitors)
- `product.photos.audit`: Audit your product photos. Assess supplied catalog product photos with fixed visual categories and report missing angles only for completely assessed full sets. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, commerce.products.list, apps.action.execute)
- `catalog.audit`: Audit your shopping catalogue. Products in the builder's shopping catalogs with missing fields, review problems or no stock. (steps: commerce.catalogs.list, commerce.products.list)
- `shipping.pickup.arrange`: Book a carrier pickup. A carrier pickup booked at a saved location for the labelled parcels waiting, with unlabelled orders listed first. (needs approval; steps: shipping.pickup-locations.list, shipping.orders.list, shipping.pickup.book)
- `shipping.label.buy`: Buy a shipping label. A label bought for an existing shipment after the address checks out and with a price limit, then the stored label read back. (needs approval; steps: geo.address.validate-detailed, shipping.label.purchase, shipping.label.get)
- `order.ship`: Buy a shipping label and notify the customer. Buy one checked shipping rate after approval, then notify its customer only on confirmed label creation. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, email.send, sms.send)
- `shipment.cancel`: Cancel a shipment. Cancel a shipment booking and its order after the live state is checked, each cancellation after approval and confirmed. (needs approval; steps: shipping.order.get, shipping.tracking.get, shipping.booking.cancel, shipping.order.cancel)
- `product.price.check`: Check current prices on marketplace products. Current price, rating and review count for a list of marketplace products, looked up one by one, with the lowest and highest price per currency and, when expected prices are given, each product against its own. (steps: amazon.product.detail)
- `marketplace.price.check`: Check marketplace prices for an item. Comparable marketplace listings for an item in a place, with a price range computed in code and the listings behind it. (steps: facebook.marketplace.locations.search, facebook.marketplace.listings.search, facebook.marketplace.listing.detail)
- `secondhand.price.check`: Check second-hand prices. Second-hand asking prices and their spread, with matching listing descriptions. (steps: xianyu.search.items, xianyu.item.detail)
- `app.rank.check`: Check your app's store search rank. Where an app ranks in each store's search for a list of terms. (steps: app-store.apps.search, google-play.apps.search)
- `catalog.sync.check`: Check your catalogue matches your store. The shopping catalogue checked against the live storefront: every catalogued product's live page read, and its name, price and stock status compared with what the page text holds. (steps: commerce.products.list, web.read, web.extract)
- `shipping.day.close`: Close out the shipping day. The end-of-day close: unlabelled orders listed, a manifest made and read back, and a pickup booked after approval. (needs approval; steps: shipping.orders.list, shipping.manifest.create, shipping.manifest.get, shipping.pickup.book)
- `shopping.price.compare`: Compare product prices by seller. Matching product prices by seller, preserving each index's quoted prices. (steps: google.serp.shopping, bing.serp.shopping, google.shopping.product)
- `china.product.compare`: Compare products on Chinese marketplaces. Exact marketplace listings with available prices, sales and shops, compared only within known currency. (steps: taobao.item.detail, douyin-shop.item.detail, xianyu.item.detail, jd.item.price, xiaohongshu.product.detail)
- `shipping.rates.compare`: Compare shipping rates. Checked carrier quotes and estimated delivery days for an existing parcel shipment, after approval. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `reviews.competitor.compare`: Compare your reviews with local competitors. A business against its local competitors: ratings, review counts and what customers praise or complain about at each. (steps: google.serp.maps, google.maps.reviews, llm.complete)
- `return.label.create`: Create a return label. A return set up with a carrier pickup from the customer, tracking registered and the customer told, each action only after approval. (needs approval; steps: shipping.order.get, shipping.return.create, shipping.exchange.create, shipping.return.update, shipping.tracking.create, email.send)
- `order.status.answer`: Draft a reply about an order's status. A checked order-status reply saved as a draft only after the inbound customer and exact app record were matched. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, email.thread.get, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `reviews.reply`: Draft replies to customer reviews. Drafted public replies to customer reviews on your business listing or your connected business page; with the post option they are posted after a human approval. (needs approval; steps: local.reviews.list, social.reviews.list, llm.complete, local.review.reply, social.reviews.reply)
- `delivery.exception.handle`: Explain a failed delivery. A failed delivery explained from the carrier's record and a customer message drafted; new instructions go to the carrier only after approval. (needs approval; steps: shipping.delivery-exception.get, shipping.order.get, email.draft.create, shipping.delivery.instructions.update)
- `catalog.barcode.fill`: Fill missing brands from barcodes. A missing brand filled in from a barcode lookup, written only where the barcode and the name both agree. (steps: commerce.products.list, commerce.barcode.lookup, commerce.product.upsert)
- `app.keyword.opportunities`: Find app store keywords you're missing. Store search terms an app does not rank for but its competitors do, with volume, ordered by likely gain. (steps: app-store.app.keywords, google-play.app.keywords, google-play.apps.shared-keywords)
- `product.price.market`: Find where a product is cheapest. Where a product is cheapest: found from a barcode or name and compared across the shops and marketplaces. (steps: commerce.barcode.lookup, commerce.prices.compare, amazon.search.products, ebay.search.products, walmart.search.products, home-depot.products.search)
- `product.listing.improve`: Improve a marketplace listing. A suggested title and bullet points for your own marketplace listing, checked line by line against your own listing; top sellers and keyword ideas are style examples only. Nothing is written back to the store. (steps: amazon.product.detail, amazon.search.products, google.keywords.ideas, llm.complete)
- `catalog.enrich`: Improve product titles and descriptions. Better titles and descriptions for the products in one of your catalogs, written from each product's own listing, written back only for the fields you name, with the values before returned so a write can be undone. (steps: commerce.products.list, llm.complete, commerce.product.upsert)
- `service.area.report`: Map a carrier's service areas. Which postal areas a carrier covers, as a file, joined to places and checked on sample addresses. (steps: shipping.postal-coverage.export, geo.postal-codes.search, shipping.serviceability.check)
- `app.competitor.map`: Map competing mobile apps. Competing apps, shared terms and each store's own ranking counts. (steps: app-store.app.competitors, google-play.app.competitors, app-store.apps.metrics, google-play.apps.metrics, google-play.apps.shared-keywords)
- `amazon.competitor.map`: Map your Amazon competitors. The products that compete with yours on Amazon, the keywords they win and where you rank against them. (steps: amazon.product.competitors, amazon.product.keywords, amazon.product.rank-overview, amazon.product.detail)
- `delivery.proof.pack`: Pack proof for a delivery dispute. An evidence pack for a delivery dispute: checkpoints, signature or photo, the order, and a written timeline. (steps: shipping.tracking.get, shipping.tracking.proof.get, shipping.order.get, llm.complete)
- `amazon.keyword.plan`: Plan keywords for an Amazon listing. A keyword plan for a listing: related terms, monthly volume, and what competitors rank for. (steps: amazon.keywords.related, amazon.product.keywords, amazon.keywords.volume, llm.complete)
- `trade.duty.quote`: Quote import duty for goods. Candidate tariff codes and EU duty readings for goods from an origin, preserving each rate and its conditions. (steps: trade.hs-codes.rank, trade.customs-duty.lookup, trade.shipments.search)
- `creator.storefront.read`: Read a creator's Amazon storefront. A creator's Amazon storefront opened up: its lists and each product's price and rating. (steps: amazon.storefront.detail, amazon.product.detail)
- `orders.recap`: Recap your orders for a period. A period summary of itemized orders: totals, taxes, top items and open states, computed in code. (steps: commerce.orders.search, commerce.order.get, llm.complete)
- `order.record.create`: Record an order. An itemized order recorded with taxes and discounts added up in code and read back to match. (needs approval; steps: commerce.products.list, commerce.order.create, commerce.order.get)
- `reputation.report`: Report on a business's reputation. Separate business rating and review samples with themes naming the reviews behind them. (steps: yelp.business.search, tripadvisor.search, trustpilot.businesses.search, google.maps.reviews, yelp.business.reviews, tripadvisor.place.reviews, app-store.app.reviews, google-play.app.reviews, llm.complete)
- `marketplace.bestsellers.report`: Report on marketplace bestsellers. The best-sellers in a category with price and rating spreads and what the leaders have in common. (steps: amazon.bestsellers.list, amazon.product.detail, llm.complete)
- `product.research`: Research products on a marketplace. Products for a search term, compared, from one online marketplace. (steps: amazon.search.products, amazon.product.detail, llm.complete)
- `tiktok.shop.market.scan`: Scan a TikTok Shop niche. A TikTok Shop niche: categories, suggested searches, matching products with price, rating and units sold, and the top few read in detail with their reviews. (steps: tiktok-shop.categories, tiktok-shop.search.suggestions, tiktok-shop.products.search, tiktok-shop.product.detail, tiktok-shop.product.reviews)
- `app.market.scan`: Scan top apps in a store category. A category's top free, paid and grossing apps and games in a country, with listings, ratings and prices, and who is new. (steps: app-store.apps.top-charts, google-play.apps.top-charts, app-store.app.detail, google-play.app.detail, google-play.games.search)
- `tiktok.shop.creator.products`: See a TikTok creator's shop products. A TikTok creator's shop showcase: profile, categories sold, and each product with price, rating and units sold. (steps: tiktok-shop.creator.profile, tiktok-shop.creator.products, tiktok-shop.product.detail)
- `checkout.shipping.options`: Show shipping options for a basket. Shipping choices for a basket before a shipment exists: can it be delivered, how soon, and what it costs. (steps: shipping.serviceability.check, shipping.services.lookup, shipping.delivery.estimate)
- `app.reviews.digest`: Summarise an app's store reviews. Themes across an app's store reviews on one or both stores, with the reviews behind each theme and the rating trend; reviewer names are left out unless asked. (steps: app-store.app.reviews, llm.complete, google-play.app.reviews)
- `china.product.reviews.digest`: Summarise Chinese marketplace reviews. What buyers say about a product on Taobao, Douyin Shop or TikTok Shop: reviews read, counted per source, and themes with the reviews behind them. (steps: taobao.item.reviews, llm.complete, douyin-shop.item.comments, tiktok-shop.product.reviews)
- `product.reviews.digest`: Summarise reviews of a business. A checked business listing's sampled review excerpts, with how many were read of how many exist, and optional sentences grounded in each individual review. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, llm.complete)
- `reviews.digest`: Summarise your customer reviews. Themes across the builder's own reviews, with a drafted reply for the unanswered low-rated ones: the reviews read from the local listing and the connected pages, themes that name the reviews behind them, and reply drafts as text. (steps: local.reviews.list, social.reviews.list, llm.complete)
- `shipment.track.notify`: Tell a customer where their parcel is. Read one parcel's matching tracking facts and draft or approve a customer text when its status changed. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, sms.send)
- `parcel.follow`: Track a parcel to delivery. A parcel followed to delivered or stuck: carriers found, tracking registered, each checkpoint logged, proof fetched on delivery. (steps: shipping.parcel.track, shipping.tracking.create, shipping.tracking.get, shipping.tracking.lookup, shipping.tracking.proof.get)
- `shipping.order.intake`: Turn a shop order into a shipping order. Turn a shop order into a shipping order after validating the address, then map products and add stock. (needs approval; steps: commerce.order.get, geo.address.validate-detailed, shipping.order.create, shipping.order.products.map, shipping.order.fulfill)
- `product.sellers.watch`: Watch who sells your product. Who sells a product and at what price and delivery terms, with undercutters flagged. (steps: amazon.product.sellers, amazon.product.detail)
