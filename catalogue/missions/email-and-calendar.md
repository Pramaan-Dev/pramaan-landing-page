# Missions: Email & calendar

Send and read email, and book, run and sum up meetings.

- `email.search.answer`: Answer a question from your mailbox. A question answered from the builder's own mailbox: the matching messages are read and the answer names the messages it rests on. (steps: email.messages.search, email.message.get, llm.complete)
- `email.list.audit`: Audit an email list for risk. Audit a list for mailbox delivery and risk signals, name concerning rows and request saved-list cleanup. (needs approval; steps: people.email.list.analyze, people.email.verify-bulk, people.email.inspect-bulk, people.email.threat.check-bulk, people.email.toxicity.check-bulk, people.email.list.get, people.email.list.remove)
- `meeting.book`: Book a meeting. Check a chosen slot on your calendar, create the meeting and email invitations after approval, then save an unsent reminder draft. (needs approval; steps: calendar.free-busy.get, calendar.event.create, email.draft.create)
- `email.template.build`: Build a reusable email template. Write reusable email copy, save a new template, verify its content and ask approval for a test sent to the builder. (needs approval; steps: email.templates.list, llm.complete, email.template.create, email.template.get, email.template.send)
- `email.domain.check`: Check a domain's email setup. What a domain publishes about its mail: mail servers and SPF, plus the reputation of the addresses its website resolves to. DMARC and DKIM are not checked. (steps: security.dns.lookup, security.domain.whois, security.ip.lookup, security.ip.reputation)
- `email.list.hygiene`: Clean a mailing list. Clean a mailing list without approval, retaining only verified unsuppressed addresses and recording confirmed bounce or complaint suppressions. (steps: email.suppressions.list, people.email.verify, email.status, email.suppression.add)
- `inbox.cleanup`: Clean up newsletters in your inbox. Without approval, find newsletters and bulk announcements, add the caller's custom label and return the old labels with an undo request. (steps: email.messages.list, email.message.clean-bulk, llm.complete, email.message.update-bulk)
- `mailbox.connect`: Connect a mailbox. Start a hosted mailbox sign-in or check it afterward, then test read-only access. (steps: email.account.connect.start, email.account.connect.status, email.messages.list, calendar.calendars.list)
- `tasks.from.email`: Create tasks from your email. Extract exact action-item wording from a bounded mail sample and create checked tasks after approval. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, email.messages.list, email.message.get, llm.complete, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute)
- `outreach.draft`: Draft a first email to a lead. A personal first email to one lead, written from their job title, their company and its news, checked sentence by sentence, and left as a draft in an inbox you already have. It never sends. (steps: companies.enrich, people.enrich, companies.news, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `meeting.schedule`: Draft a meeting invite. Find a candidate free on every supplied calendar and store an invite draft; availability is a snapshot. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, email.draft.create)
- `outreach.reply.draft`: Draft a reply to a prospect. A drafted answer to a prospect's reply, written from the thread and your context, checked sentence by sentence, and stored as a new draft in the same inbox. The recipient and the subject come from the thread's headers. A draft is written into your mailbox; nothing is sent. (steps: email.thread.get, companies.enrich, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `reply.draft`: Draft a reply to an email. A drafted reply to one email, written from its thread and the builder's notes and checked sentence by sentence, saved as a draft in the inbox. Nothing is sent. (steps: email.thread.get, memory.search, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `outreach.sequence.draft`: Draft a three-step email sequence. A three-step email sequence for one lead, written from their company and its news, checked sentence by sentence, and left as drafts in an inbox you already have. Nothing is sent. (steps: companies.enrich, companies.news, llm.complete, email.draft.create)
- `meeting.find-slot`: Find a time everyone is free. Find the earliest supplied meeting slot confirmed free on every calendar during each participant's local business hours. (steps: calendar.free-busy.get, time.timezone.lookup, time.business-day.check)
- `email.deliverability.fix`: Fix your email deliverability. Repair an existing owned sending domain, check authentication, and send an approved test. (needs approval; steps: domains.registration.get, email.domain.get, email.domain.zonefile.get, domains.dns-records.apply, email.domain.verify, security.domain.email-auth, email.send)
- `meeting.followup`: Follow up after a meeting. A checked transcript summary stored as a draft, confirmed task creations and any next-meeting proposal stated on the call. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, audio.transcription.create, llm.complete, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, email.draft.create)
- `inbox.digest`: Get a daily digest of your inbox. A daily summary of every conversation that moved: the day's threads are read and each gets one or two sentences on what moved and what is open, checked against that thread alone. Nothing is sent or changed. (steps: email.threads.list, email.thread.get, llm.complete)
- `time.send-window.pick`: Pick a good time to send. Choose the earliest supplied send instant inside local business hours, after business-day and complete holiday checks. (steps: time.timezone.lookup, time.business-day.check, time.holidays.list)
- `time.convert.plan`: Plan a meeting across time zones. Plan one UTC meeting across named timezones and return each requested unit conversion unchanged. (steps: time.date.parse, time.timezone.lookup, units.convert)
- `daily.agenda`: Plan your day from calendar and mail. Today's meetings in the caller's time zone, explicitly urgent sampled mail and checked open tasks in one view. (needs approval; steps: apps.connections.list, apps.catalog.search, apps.action.execute, email.threads.list, llm.complete)
- `email.attachment.extract`: Pull fields from email attachments. Fields pulled from one message's attachments: each readable attachment is parsed to text and extracted, and a value is returned only when it occurs in that text. Executables, macro files, archives and oversize files are refused unread. (steps: email.message.get, email.attachment.get, document.parse, document.extract)
- `email.reply.send`: Reply to an email thread. Draft a checked thread reply from notes, ask approval, send it and read back its mailbox copy. (needs approval; steps: email.thread.get, llm.complete, email.message.reply, email.message.get)
- `meeting.reschedule`: Reschedule a meeting. Read an owned one-off meeting, choose the first free candidate slot, and approve an update that emails attendees. (needs approval; steps: calendar.event.get, calendar.free-busy.get, calendar.event.update)
- `meeting.rsvp.handle`: Respond to a meeting invite. Read an invitation and check calendar conflicts before approving an emailed RSVP; acceptance is withheld when busy or unchecked. (needs approval; steps: calendar.event.get, calendar.agenda.get, calendar.event.respond)
- `email.attachments.save`: Save email attachments. Read matching mail attachments and store original bytes and readable text copies in a private bucket, with approval for each storage write. (needs approval; steps: email.messages.search, storage.buckets.list, email.attachment.get, email.attachment.text.get, storage.object.put)
- `email.send-tracked`: Send a tracked email. One email sent from your connected mail account, behind an approval, and followed to its delivery state: queued, sent, delivered, bounced or another state the account reports. Sent as plain text exactly as given: opens and clicks are not tracked and nothing tracking is added. (needs approval; steps: email.send, email.status)
- `email.bulk.send`: Send personalised emails in bulk. Preview and approve personalised messages, skip suppressed addresses and report each delivery snapshot and new bounce suppression. (needs approval; steps: email.suppressions.list, email.send-bulk, email.status, email.suppression.add, email.metrics.get)
- `email.domain.setup`: Set up a sending domain for email. A sending domain added to your connected mail account, its DNS records compared with what is live, and the account's own verdict read back: verified, pending or failed. (steps: email.domain.verify, security.dns.lookup)
- `outreach.replies.triage`: Sort replies to your outreach. Sort replies to a sequence into interested, not now, wrong person, out of office and unsubscribe, and list the people who asked to stop. (steps: email.threads.list, email.thread.get, outreach.contacts.list, llm.complete)
- `inbox.triage`: Sort your newest mail by urgency. The newest mail in one inbox, each message read, sorted by kind and urgency with a checked one-line summary and a caution, set in code, on mail that asks for money, credentials, secrecy or urgency. Nothing is sent or changed. (steps: email.messages.list, email.message.get, llm.complete)
- `meeting.summarize`: Summarise a meeting. Summary, decisions and action items from one recording, every item anchored to the transcript passage it came from, with its timestamp. (steps: audio.transcription.create, llm.complete)
- `thread.summarize`: Summarise an email thread. One conversation in one inbox: who said what and what is open, with a timeline and participants built from the thread and a written summary checked sentence by sentence against it. Nothing is sent or changed. (steps: email.thread.get, llm.complete)
- `email.thread.forward-summary`: Summarise and forward an email thread. Summarise a thread and forward its named message with the checked summary above it, after approval. (needs approval; steps: email.thread.get, email.message.summary.get, llm.complete, email.message.forward)
