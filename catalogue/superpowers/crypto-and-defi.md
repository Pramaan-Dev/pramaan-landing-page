# Superpowers: Crypto & DeFi

Tokens, wallets, chains, DeFi yields and on-chain data.

## Crypto derivatives

See futures, options, funding rates, open interest and liquidations.

- `crypto.derivatives.overview`: Trading volume of derivatives exchanges, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.derivatives.protocol`: Trading volume of one derivatives exchange, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.futures.overview`: Futures market figures (open interest, funding, volume) across tracked tokens, ranked.
- `crypto.liquidations.by-exchange`: Liquidations broken down by exchange, ranked.
- `crypto.liquidations.chart`: Liquidations of a token on an exchange over time, as long and short totals per period.
- `crypto.liquidations.large-orders`: Individual large liquidation orders above a size in US dollars.
- `crypto.open-interest.overview`: Open interest across derivatives exchanges, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.options.market`: Options market figures for a crypto asset, such as open interest, volume and put to call ratio.
- `crypto.options.overview`: Trading volume of options exchanges, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.options.protocol`: Trading volume of one options exchange, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.perp.funding-history`: The funding rate history of a perpetual futures contract on an exchange.
- `crypto.perp.funding-rates`: Funding rates and open interest of perpetual futures across exchanges.
- `crypto.perp.long-short`: The ratio of long to short positions over time for a perpetual futures contract.
- `crypto.perp.snapshot`: A snapshot of a perpetual futures contract, with its funding rate, open interest and prices.

## Crypto funding and funds

Find crypto funding rounds, venture funds, token sales and listed treasuries.

- `crypto.entities.list`: The entities (companies and funds) that hold or run crypto assets tracked by the source, with their holdings.
- `crypto.fund.portfolio`: The funding rounds a crypto venture fund took part in, newest first.
- `crypto.fund.profile`: A crypto venture fund's profile, with its description, jurisdiction, tier and portfolio size.
- `crypto.fundraising.search`: Find crypto fundraising and token sale events, or the latest ones.
- `crypto.funds.ranking`: The leading crypto venture funds, ranked by tier or by portfolio size.
- `crypto.funds.search`: Find crypto venture funds by keyword.
- `crypto.raises.list`: Crypto fundraising rounds, with amount, round type, lead investors and date.
- `crypto.token.generation-event`: Whether and when a crypto project held its token generation event.
- `crypto.token.public-sale`: The public sale (ICO, IDO or IEO) of a crypto project, with its price, raise and dates.
- `crypto.treasury-companies.list`: Listed companies that hold crypto on their balance sheet, with their holdings.
- `crypto.treasury-company.detail`: One listed company's crypto treasury, with its holdings and purchases.

## Crypto prices and markets

Check coin prices, exchange order books, candles, ETFs and market mood.

- `crypto.coin.profile`: A crypto coin's profile by its id, with its description, categories, launch date and current price and market value in US dollars.
- `crypto.dex.token-price`: Price bars (open, high, low, close, volume) of a token across decentralised exchange pools, by contract address and chain.
- `crypto.etf.flows`: Daily inflows and outflows of spot crypto exchange-traded funds.
- `crypto.etf.snapshot`: Crypto exchange-traded funds with their assets, flows and fees.
- `crypto.exchange.candles`: Historical price candles of a spot market on a Korean or Japanese crypto exchange.
- `crypto.exchange.klines`: Price candles of a trading pair on an exchange, with a summary of the period's high, low and volume.
- `crypto.exchange.markets-coverage`: The spot markets covered on several Asian crypto exchanges, with their latest price and 24-hour change.
- `crypto.exchange.order-book`: The order book of a trading pair on an exchange, with bid and ask levels and spread.
- `crypto.exchange.pairs`: The trading pairs listed on a crypto exchange.
- `crypto.exchange.ticker`: The live ticker of a trading pair on a crypto exchange, with price, change and volume.
- `crypto.exchanges.list`: List crypto exchanges by trading volume, with their country, year founded and trust score.
- `crypto.fear-greed.get`: The crypto market fear and greed index, from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed), with its trend.
- `crypto.listings.events`: Historical crypto exchange listing and delisting events.
- `crypto.market.global`: The whole crypto market at a glance - total market value, how much bitcoin and ether make up, and how many coins and markets there are.
- `crypto.market.rankings`: The largest crypto coins by market value, with price, 24-hour range and change, priced in the currency you choose.
- `crypto.market.trending`: The coins most searched for in the last 24 hours, with their price and how it moved.
- `crypto.price.currencies`: The currencies crypto prices can be quoted in.
- `crypto.price.history`: A crypto token's price over a time range.
- `crypto.price.ohlc`: Read public open, high, low and close price candles for one crypto currency pair. Returns up to fifty candles.
- `crypto.search`: Find crypto coins by name or symbol, with the id other crypto capabilities take.
- `crypto.technical.indicator`: A technical indicator (such as RSI or MACD) for a trading pair on an exchange and interval.
- `crypto.token.prices`: Read current or historical US-dollar prices and percentage changes for public token contracts.

## DeFi protocols

Track DeFi protocols, chains, bridges and exchanges by value, fees and volume.

- `crypto.bridges.day-stats`: A day's bridge volume on a chain, broken down by token and address.
- `crypto.bridges.list`: Cross-chain bridges with their recent daily, weekly and monthly volume.
- `crypto.bridges.ranking`: Cross-chain bridge protocols ranked by volume in US dollars over a period.
- `crypto.bridges.volume-history`: Daily bridge deposit and withdrawal volume on a chain.
- `crypto.chains.assets`: The assets held on each blockchain, by kind (canonical, native, bridged), in US dollars.
- `crypto.chains.tvl`: The value locked on each blockchain now.
- `crypto.chains.tvl-history`: Historical value locked in DeFi across all chains or one chain, in US dollars. Returns up to 50 observations in source order.
- `crypto.defi.metric-history`: One metric of a DeFi protocol (such as daily value locked or fees) over time.
- `crypto.defi.metric-overview`: Totals and changes of a DeFi metric (such as fees, revenue or DEX volume) over the last day, week, month and year, for all of DeFi or one category, chain or protocol.
- `crypto.defi.ranking`: DeFi protocols ranked by value locked, fees or revenue.
- `crypto.dex.activity`: Traders, trades and volume for a decentralised exchange protocol over a period.
- `crypto.dex.summary`: Trading volume of one decentralised exchange, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.dexs.overview`: Trading volume of decentralised exchanges, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.fees.overview`: Fees and revenue of DeFi protocols, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.fees.protocol`: Fees and revenue of one DeFi protocol, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.fork.overview`: Value locked in forks of DeFi protocols, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.hacks.list`: Recorded crypto hacks and exploits, with the amount taken, chain, technique and funds returned.
- `crypto.protocol.detail`: A DeFi protocol's profile and value locked, with its chains, category and links.
- `crypto.protocol.financials`: A DeFi protocol's income statement (revenue, costs, earnings) by period.
- `crypto.protocol.flows`: The inflows and outflows of a DeFi protocol over the day after a given time.
- `crypto.protocol.treasury`: A DeFi protocol's treasury value and holdings, with totals and changes.
- `crypto.protocol.tvl`: Read the current total USD value locked in one DeFi protocol.
- `crypto.protocol.tvl-metrics`: A DeFi protocol's value locked, with totals and changes over time.
- `crypto.protocols.list`: DeFi protocols with their value locked, category, chains and recent change.
- `crypto.treasuries.list`: DeFi protocol treasuries, with their value and holdings.

## DeFi yields and borrowing

Compare yields, staking rates and borrowing costs across DeFi.

- `crypto.borrow.markets`: Lending markets with the latest borrowing rates and amounts.
- `crypto.borrow.pools`: Borrowing costs of assets on lending markets, with annual rates and value locked.
- `crypto.borrow.routes`: Ways to borrow an asset across lending markets, with the latest rates.
- `crypto.earn.pools`: Earning pools (staking, lending, liquidity) with the latest yields.
- `crypto.earn.verified-pools`: Earning pools whose yields the source has measured and verified.
- `crypto.liquid-staking.rates`: Exchange rates of liquid staking tokens against ether, and how far each is off its peg.
- `crypto.yield.loops`: Looping strategies (borrowing against a deposit to deposit again), with their net yields.
- `crypto.yield.pools`: DeFi yield pools ranked by annual yield or by value locked.
- `crypto.yield.pools-list`: DeFi yield pools with their annual yield, value locked, chain and project.

## Hyperliquid

- `crypto.hyperliquid.account`: A wallet's portfolio on the Hyperliquid exchange across perpetuals, spot, vaults and staking.
- `crypto.hyperliquid.candles`: Price candles of a Hyperliquid market.
- `crypto.hyperliquid.fills`: A wallet's individual trade fills on Hyperliquid, newest first.
- `crypto.hyperliquid.orders`: A wallet's open orders on Hyperliquid (resting, stop, take-profit and limit).
- `crypto.hyperliquid.performance`: Read public account balances and trading performance on Hyperliquid. Returns personal data: Public names, profiles, wallet activity or published records; see the result fields..
- `crypto.hyperliquid.positions`: A wallet's open perpetual positions on Hyperliquid, with its margin summary.
- `crypto.hyperliquid.trade-context`: The funding paid and leverage used over one closed Hyperliquid trade.
- `crypto.hyperliquid.trades`: A wallet's closed trades on Hyperliquid, one row per position from open to close.
- `crypto.hyperliquid.trades-summary`: A wallet's closed trades on Hyperliquid rolled up into one row per group (such as market or day).

## Wallets and on-chain

Look into wallets, tokens, transfers and transactions straight from the blockchain.

- `crypto.address.validate`: Check that an Ethereum address is well formed and its checksum is right, and return its normalised form.
- `crypto.block.at-time`: The block of a chain closest to a given time.
- `crypto.ens.resolve`: Resolve an ENS name to its Ethereum address.
- `crypto.gas.price`: The current gas price of an EVM chain.
- `crypto.onchain.indicator`: An on-chain indicator over time for bitcoin or ether.
- `crypto.onchain.sql`: Run a read-only SQL query against indexed blockchain tables and get the rows back.
- `crypto.onchain.sql-estimate`: Check a read-only SQL query against the blockchain tables and estimate how much data it reads, without running it.
- `crypto.token.counterparties`: The addresses that received or sent the most of a token over a period.
- `crypto.token.dex-trades`: Recent decentralised exchange swaps of a token, by contract address.
- `crypto.token.holders`: The largest holders of a token, by contract address.
- `crypto.token.protocols`: How much of a token each DeFi protocol holds.
- `crypto.token.resolve`: Turn a token ticker symbol into its likely contract addresses on supported chains.
- `crypto.token.transfer-stats`: A summary of a token's transfer activity over a period, with counts, volume and active addresses.
- `crypto.token.transfers`: Recent transfers of a token, by contract address.
- `crypto.traders.leaderboard`: List public wallet trading totals on a selected market. Returns personal data: Public names, profiles, wallet activity or published records; see the result fields..
- `crypto.transaction.detail`: One blockchain transaction by its hash, with sender, receiver, value, fee and status.
- `crypto.wallet.age`: When a wallet first moved funds on a chain and how old it is in days.
- `crypto.wallet.balance`: A wallet's native coin balance on a chain and the tokens it recently moved.
- `crypto.wallet.defi-positions`: A wallet's positions in DeFi protocols, such as lending, staking and liquidity.
- `crypto.wallet.history`: A wallet's on-chain transactions, each classified as a send, receive, swap and so on.
- `crypto.wallet.labels`: Look up public entity labels attached to wallet addresses. Returns personal data: Public names, profiles, wallet activity or published records; see the result fields..
- `crypto.wallet.net-worth`: A wallet's total value in US dollars over time.
- `crypto.wallet.portfolio`: A wallet's holdings across chains, with balances and token values.
- `crypto.wallet.token-transfers`: A wallet's token transfers with token, US dollar value and direction.
- `crypto.wallet.transactions`: A wallet's recent transactions on a chain, with sent and received counts.
- `crypto.wallets.search`: Find wallets by ENS name, label or address prefix.
