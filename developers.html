FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account with each provider?↓

No. One Pramaan key covers every provider. If you already pay one, connect your own account and calls through it are free.

How is this different from treg or Monid?↓

They're providers inside Pramaan. Aggregators hand you thousands of raw endpoints; Pramaan turns them, and direct providers, into typed capabilities with neutral results, failover, approvals and verification status.

What does live-verified mean?↓

A real call to that provider returned the expected shape, and we logged it with its cost. Capabilities that are only built or spec-checked are labelled as such.

How is a call priced?↓

The provider's cost plus 30%, with a $0.001 minimum, drawn from a prepaid balance. Every new organisation gets $1.00 to start.

Can my agent send an email or take a payment on its own?↓

Not without a yes. Capabilities that act outside are classed as reversible or irreversible, and the ones that need it ask for explicit approval first.

How do I get a key?↓

During early access, keys are issued by the Pramaan team. Request access and we'll set up your organisation.
